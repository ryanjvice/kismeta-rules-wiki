---
title: "Objective"
description: "Ascend through seven chakras before the Aethereal Veil claims you."
---

Ascend through your seven chakras to gain enlightenment before you are corrupted by the chaotic energy of the Aethereal Veil. Each round, build a three-card spread and try to outscore the Table's hand without going over the **50-point threshold**. Victories move you upward on your ascension path while defeats add to your corruption level.
