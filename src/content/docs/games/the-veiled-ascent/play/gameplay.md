---
title: "Gameplay Steps"
description: "Deal the Veil, Build Your Spread, Reveal & Resolve — the three steps of every round."
---

*Gameplay is the same regardless of the number of players.*

### Step 1: Deal the Veil *(Dealer/AI)*

Deal 3 cards for the Table:
- First deal **1 face-up**
- Then deal **2 face-down**
- These cards stay hidden until Step 4.

---

### Step 2: Build Your Spread *(Player)*

Draw 4 cards:
- First deal **1 face-down** — this card stays hidden until Step 4
- Then deal **3 face-up**

Review your revealed cards. You will be adding the value of your unrevealed card in the next step.

**Discard 1 face-up card.**

> **Your final Spread = 2 face-up cards + your face-down card.**

---

### Step 3: Reveal & Resolve

**Say aloud:** *"The Veil is lifted."*

1. **Reveal** all face-down cards (Table's and yours)
2. **Check** for 3-card patterns in your Spread (announce if you have one)
3. **Calculate** each side's total (do not exceed 50)
4. **Apply** pattern effects and determine outcome
5. **Move** tokens and adjust Corruption accordingly
