---
title: "Round Outcomes"
description: "How advancing, tying, and losing affect your ascension."
---

| Outcome | Result |
|---------|--------|
| Your total > Table | Advance 1 space |
| Exact 50 | Advance 2 spaces |
| Table busts (you don't) | Advance 1 space & remove 1 Corruption |
| Tie | No movement or penalty |
| Loss (Table wins or you Overreach) | Move to Corruption Zone, +1 Corruption |
| Lose again in Corruption Zone | Stay in place, +1 Corruption |

> **Note:** Pattern bonuses may modify these outcomes — see [Pattern Bonuses](/games/the-veiled-ascent/reference/pattern-bonuses/) below.
