## The Zodiac Wheel

Every Sign on the Zodiac Wheel carries three Aspects: itself, its ruling Planet, and its Element. Each Sign also carries a number from 1 to 12, which is how the Cosmic Age Die and your Zodiac Die select it.

| # | SIGN         | RULING PLANET | ELEMENT | CARD SUIT     |
| - | ------------ | ------------- | ------- | ------------- |
| 1 | Aries        | Mars          | Fire    | 🪄 Wands      |
| 2 | Taurus       | Venus         | Earth   | 🪙 Pentacles  |
| 3 | Gemini       | Mercury       | Air     | ⚔️ Swords     |
| 4 | Cancer       | Moon          | Water   | 🍷 Cups       |
| 5 | Leo          | Sun           | Fire    | 🪄 Wands      |
| 6 | Virgo        | Mercury       | Earth   | 🪙 Pentacles  |
| 7 | Libra        | Venus         | Air     | ⚔️ Swords     |
| 8 | Scorpio      | Mars          | Water   | 🍷 Cups       |
| 9 | Sagittarius  | Jupiter       | Fire    | 🪄 Wands      |
| 10 | Capricorn   | Saturn        | Earth   | 🪙 Pentacles  |
| 11 | Aquarius    | Saturn        | Air     | ⚔️ Swords     |
| 12 | Pisces      | Jupiter       | Water   | 🍷 Cups       |

**Harvest values** for each Aspect are printed on the Zodiac Wheel: Sign +3, Planet +2, Element +1, and a Perfect Match (all three at once) scores the full stack of +6. Each point pays 1 Essence.

📌 **Note on valid pairs:** the table above is also the list of legal Planet and Element combinations. A Crucible Card in Group C calls for a pair that appears here. A Group D card calls for a pair that does not, such as Sun with Water, which no single Sign carries. That is why Gold Stage Formulas cannot be satisfied from your Zodiac Die alone.

## Correspondence

Every Sign, card suit, Furnace, Reagent, and Essence corresponds to one of the four Elements.

| ELEMENT | ZODIAC SIGNS               | CARD SUIT | REAGENT     | FURNACE     |
| ------- | -------------------------- | --------- | ----------- | ----------- |
| Fire    | Aries, Leo, Sagittarius    | 🪄 Wands  | Sulfur      | 🔴 Red      |
| Earth   | Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn   | 🪙 Pentacles | Vitriol  | 🟢 Green    |
| Air     | Gemini, Libra, Aquarius    | ⚔️ Swords | Quicksilver | 🟡 Yellow   |
| Water   | Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces    | 🍷 Cups   | Aqua Regia  | 🔵 Blue     |

A Furnace's color describes the Furnace itself, never its occupants. Coal is always in its owner's player color.

## Stelliums

Every Minor Arcana card carries a Planet. Hold **three cards of one Planet** together in your **Spread** to activate that Planet's **Stellium**. The effect holds only while all three remain: losing one to a Duel switches it off.

Only Spread cards count. Hand cards and Arcanum cards never contribute to a Stellium. An Ace is wild for any one Stellium. One Ace per Stellium.

| Planet        | Ranks         | While Active                                                          |
| ------------- | ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ☽ **Moon**    | 2, Queen      | Your Coal cannot be Extinguished.                                     |
| ☿ **Mercury** | 5, Princess   | You may fuel any Furnace, not only the one matching your Sign.        |
| ♀ **Venus**   | 4, 9          | At Harvest, take your Essence in any one Element you choose.          |
| ♂ **Mars**    | 7, Knight     | Losing a Duel does not bar you from challenging that rival again this Age. |
| ♃ **Jupiter** | 3, 8, King    | You may build an Astral House on the **Cosmic Age**, not only your own Sign. |
| ♄ **Saturn**  | 6, 10         | Your Spread limit is 9 instead of 7.                                  |

## Card Effect Verbs

_Shared rules language printed on Minor and Major Arcana cards._

| VERB            | MEANING                                                                                                                              |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Burn**        | Discard this card to use the effect that follows.                                                                                    |
| **Distill**     | Spend 5 Essence of one Element to take the Reagent of that Element.                                                                  |
| **Extinguish**  | Remove one Coal from a Furnace. It returns to its owner's supply and their Heat in that Element drops by 1. A Ward can stop it.      |
| **Neutralize**  | Cancel the Reversed effect of a card in your Spread. Both cards remain in play.                                                      |
| **Reversed**    | A penalty that applies only while this card is in your Spread. Moving it to your Hand, or Neutralizing it, ends the penalty.         |
| **Scry X**      | Look at the top X cards of the Kismeta Deck, or a rival's Hand, as the card states. What you learn is yours to keep.                 |
| **Sever**       | Switch off one card's printed text until Transit, as the card states.                                                                |
| **Ward**        | The warded card, Stone, or resource cannot be targeted, as the card states.                                                          |

## Crucible Cards

The Crucible Deck (red back) holds 22 cards across four Groups. Each card is one Transmutation Stage, and its face shows a full Alchemical Formula: an Attunement, a Reagent cost, and a Furnace Heat requirement.

**Attunement** is paid from Spread cards, which are spent, or from Astral Houses, which are not. An Astral House fills one slot of any Aspect it carries, once per Firing.

## Major Arcana

The 22 Major Arcana cards are divided into two sets.

**Fates** are volatile, disruptive cosmic events that arrive uninvited.  
**Adepts** are persistent permissions the Alchemist actively acquires and buys into.

| | **FATE CARDS (10)** | **ADEPT CARDS (12)** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Effect Duration** | Resolve on draw. Some hold until Transit | Persistent for as long as the card is held |
| **Aspects** | None, effect only | Carry all 3 Aspects (Sign, Element, Planet) |
| **Entry** | Resolves the instant it is drawn | Purchased for 3 Essence, of any type |
| **Tiers** | Single effect | Base and Resonant |
| **Arcanum Limit** | No limit | Max 2 cards |
| **Player Agency** | Low, forced on all or the drawer | High, player chooses to buy in |
| **Design Role** | Disruption, equalization, social moments | Specialization, optimization, identity |

### Planet Stellium Codex

Hold three cards of one Planet in your Spread to activate its Stellium. It ends the moment one of those cards is lost.

| Planet | Ranks | While Active |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ☽ **Moon** | 2, Queen | Your Coal cannot be Extinguished. |
| ☿ **Mercury** | 5, Princess | You may fuel any Furnace. |
| ♀ **Venus** | 4, 9 | Take your Harvest in any Essence you choose. |
| ♂ **Mars** | 7, Knight | Losing a Duel doesn't bar you from re-challenging. |
| ♃ **Jupiter** | 3, 8, King | You may build an Astral House on the Cosmic Age. |
| ♄ **Saturn** | 6, 10 | Your Spread limit is 9 instead of 7. |

An Ace is wild for any one Stellium. One Ace per Stellium.
