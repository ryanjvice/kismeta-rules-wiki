## In the Box

**Board & Cards**

- **1 Great Year Board** (includes the Zodiac Wheel, the Mantle Ring, the Forge, Stasis, the Altar of Kismeta, the four shared Furnaces, the **Bazaar**, and a Codex panel for each player showing the Coal and Reagent costs)
- **Kismeta Card Deck** (134 cards, blue back: 112 Minor Arcana + 22 Major Arcana: 12 Adept, 10 Fate)
- **Crucible Card Deck** (22 cards, red back: Groups A, B, C, D; 4 per player, per game)

**Dice**

- **1 Cosmic Age Die** (12-sided, black; rolled by the Agekeeper each Age)
- **4 Zodiac Dice** (12-sided, 1 per player in matching player color: Red, Green, Blue, White)

**Player Pieces** (per player, in 4 colors: Red, Green, Blue, White)

- **4 Philosopher's Stones** (1 per player)
- **4 Meeples** (1 per player; marks your current Zodiac Sign on the board)
- **16 Astral Houses** (4 per player; small wooden cylinder token)

**Tokens & Resources**

- **24 Coal** (6 per player; wooden discs in each player's color; fuel for the Furnaces)
- **Reagents** (punchout tokens, one type per Element):
  🔴 Sulfur (Fire) | 🟢 Vitriol (Earth) | 🟡 Quicksilver (Air) | 🔵 Aqua Regia (Water)
- **Essence** (112 paper bills; the game's base currency) in four Elements: Fire, Earth, Air, and Water
- **1 Agekeeper's Key** (black pawn)

## Anatomy of the Great Year Board

Every ring of the Great Work is printed on the board. Working from the outside in:

- **The Zodiac Wheel.** Twelve Signs ring the board. Each Sign lists its three Aspects and what each is worth when you Align with it: +1 for its Element, +2 for its Planet, and +3 for the Sign itself. The 🏰 icon on each Sign marks where an Astral House can be built.
- **The Number Ring.** Each Sign bears a number from 1 to 12, matching the faces of the Cosmic Age Die and your Zodiac Die. When either die is rolled, it is placed on the Sign bearing that number.
- **The Mantle Ring.** Four sections, one per player, each in that player's color. Your section holds your START space, your Mantle positions 2, 4, and 6, and a Stasis space marked with a snowflake ❄️. A Stone resting on the Mantle is safe and cannot be targeted in a Siege.
- **The Forge.** Four stations at the heart of the board, passed through in order: Lead (1), Bronze (3), Silver (5), and Gold (7). There is only one of each, so every Alchemist competes for the same four stations. A Stone in the Forge is working through a Transmutation Stage, and is exposed to Siege for the single Summer it spends there.
- **The Altar of Kismeta.** The center of the board, position 8, marks the end of the Great Work. The first Stone to arrive wins the game.
- **The Furnaces.** Four rosettes at the corners of the board, one for each suit and Element: Wands and Fire (red), Cups and Water (blue), Swords and Air (yellow), and Pentacles and Earth (green). Coal is placed here to raise your Heat. Each Furnace holds three Coal in total, shared among all players and claimed first come, first served.
- **The Bazaar.** A shared reference area on the board. It lists every fixed exchange rate in the game, so no one has to hunt through the rules mid-turn.
- **Codex Panels.** The four framed panels along the board's edges are your working areas. Your Codex lists the Coal and Reagent costs, and the space around it holds your Tableau: your Spread, your Hand, and your Arcanum.
