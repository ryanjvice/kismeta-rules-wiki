Reagents are the concentrated alchemical fuels required to power the Great Work. While Common Cards represent raw potential gathered from the cosmos, Reagents are the refined energy needed to "Fuel the Forge" and advance your Philosopher’s Stone through the Crucible.

# FOUR REAGENTS
There are four types of Reagents, each refined from a specific suit of Common Cards:

| REAGENT | REQ’D SUIT TO REFINE | CAULDRON |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Sulphur | Wands | Red |
| Quicksilver | Swords | Yellow |
| Aqua Regia | Cups | Blue |
| Vitriol | Pentacles | Green |

# REFINING REAGENTS
Refinement is the process of trading in cards to receive a Reagent token. You may do this any number of times during Phase 2: Summer.

| # OF COALS IN A CAULDRON | RP THRESHOLD (Per Suit) |
| :--- | :--- |
| 0 | ≥ 30 RP |
| 1 | ≥ 25 RP |
| 2 | ≥ 20 RP |
| 3 | ≥ 15 RP |
| 4 | ≥ 10 RP |

* **The Threshold:** To create one Reagent, you must discard a set of cards of the matching suit whose combined Rank Points (RP) meet or exceed the current Total Rank Sum Threshold. The default Threshold for all Reagents is 30 Rank Points. This can be lowered by 5 RP for every Molten Coal placed in the corresponding Crucible Cauldron on the game board.
* **Using Your Full Inventory:** You may use cards from your Spread and your Hand to reach the required point total.
* **The Exchange:** Once the Threshold is met, discard the cards to the common pile and take one matching Reagent token from the supply.

💡 **Tip:** Each Molten Coal placed in a Cauldron permanently lowers that Cauldron’s Threshold by 5 RP each. They cannot be moved once placed. Choose wisely.

# EXAMPLE: Refining A Reagent
You want to refine 1 Sulphur (Red) Reagent.
Your Red Cauldron has 2 Molten Coals, lowering the Threshold to 20 RP.
Your Cards:
* 6 of Wands = 6 RP
* 8 of Wands = 8 RP
* Knight of Wands = 11 RP
* Total RP: 25 RP: satisfies the 20 RP Threshold.
Result: Discard all three cards and take 1 Sulphur token from the supply.

If you fall short: If you only held the 6 and 8 of Wands (14 RP), you fall short and cannot refine. Acquire more Wands cards before attempting again.
