# In the Box

**Board & Cards**

- **1 Great Year Board**
- **134 Common Cards** (112 Minor Arcana + 22 Major Arcana: 11 Specialist, 11 Standard)
- **22 Crucible Cards** (Groups A, B, C, D; 4 per player; per game)
- **4 Crucible Codex Cards** (1 per player; dual-sided: Codex + Quick Reference Guide)

**Dice**

- **1 Cosmic Age Die** (12-sided, black; rolled by the Agekeeper each round)
- **4 Zodiac Dice** (12-sided, 1 per player in matching player color: Red, Green, Blue, White)

**Player Pieces** (per player, in 4 colors: Red, Green, Blue, White)

- **4 Philosopher's Stones** (1 per player)
- **4 Meeples** (1 per player; marks your current Zodiac Sign on the board)
- **16 Astral House Tokens** (4 per player; 🏰 symbol)
- **1 Cauldron Catalyst** per player (4 total)

**Tokens & Resources**

- **96 Reagent Tokens** (circle punchouts, 24 of each color):
  🔴 Sulphur (Red) | 🟡 Quicksilver (Yellow) | 🔵 Aqua Regia (Blue) | 🟢 Vitriol (Green)
- **16 Molten Coals** (4 per player; small black crystal pieces)
- **1 Agekeeper's Key** (black pawn)
