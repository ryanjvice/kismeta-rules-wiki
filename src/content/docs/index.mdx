## How to use this site

<CardGrid stagger>
  <Card title="Search" icon="magnifier">
    Press **Ctrl+K** (or **⌘K** on Mac) to find any rule, term, or phase instantly.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Four tabs" icon="list-format">
    **Learn** for lore and overview, **Play** for setup and rounds, **Reference** for the compendium, **Glossary** for quick definitions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Share a rule" icon="external">
    Click a page heading to copy a direct link — send it to your table when a dispute comes up.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Install offline" icon="setting">
    Use your browser’s **Install app** option (or Add to Home Screen on mobile). Works at the table without Wi‑Fi after the first visit.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Game mode modifiers" icon="caution">
    Toggle **Quickplay** and **Magnus** modifiers in the header to show or hide ⚙️ rule variants.
  </Card>
  <Card title="New players" icon="open-book">
    Read [Game Overview](/learn/game-overview/), then [Setup](/play/setup/), then keep [Round at a Glance](/play/round-at-a-glance/) handy during play.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
