# Alchemists of the Great Year

## Guided Playbook

2–4 players · 60–120 minutes · _A GOODMAGIK GAME_

> A table companion for learning and running your first Cosmic Ages. Follow each step in order as play unfolds.

## Part 1 — Learn the Game

### Step 1: Gather Round the Crucible of Kismeta, Dear Alchemists!

Today, you and your rival Alchemists will race to be the first to complete the **Great Work**, that arcane ritual of transforming your Philosopher's Stone from Lead into Gold. This race takes place across the many Cosmic Ages of the Great Year.

Each Age moves through **Spring → Summer → Autumn → Winter**. Follow each step in this guide in order as play unfolds at the table.

Before you begin, learn the shape of a Cosmic Age and how you will achieve the Great Work.

- [ ] Complete the Great Work (Lead → Gold) before your rivals
- [ ] Harvest Essence, fuel Furnaces, and advance your Stone each Autumn
- [ ] Build your Spread, Hand, and Arcanum across the Ages
- [ ] Work through each step at the table as the Age unfolds

### Step 2: The Shape of a Cosmic Age

Every round of Kismeta is one **Cosmic Age** of the Great Year. Each Age follows the same four-season pattern, repeating until someone completes the Great Work.

Learn the rhythm now; you will walk through each season step by step once play begins.

Each Cosmic Age moves through four seasons in order. Return here during play for a quick reference.

| 🌸 Spring | ☀️ Summer | 🍂 Autumn | ⛰️ Winter |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *Set the Age, Deal the Cards & Build Your Tableau* | *Craft, Distill, Trade & Contest* | *Harvest, Fuel & Conduct the Great Work* | *Final Actions & Reset* |
| Set the Cosmic Age, take the Gift of the Age, roll your Sign, receive 3 Kismeta cards, build your Tableau, and lock cards until Winter. | Craft Houses and Mansions, Distill Reagents, trade with rivals, Duel for cards, Extinguish Coal, and Siege exposed Stones. | Harvest Essence by Alignment, fuel Furnaces with Coal, then Fire, Temper, or Rekindle your Stone as the Great Work demands. | Unlock cards, optionally wager on the next Age, enforce limits, then Transit: shuffle the discard pile and pass the Key. |

### Step 3: Tools of the Alchemist

Before you learn how Essence and cards fuel the Great Work, learn the **tools** every Alchemist uses at the table — the names every step ahead will assume you know.

These are the tools you will use every Cosmic Age. Learn them now — every step ahead assumes you know these names.

- **Cosmic Age Die & Zodiac Die** — Each Spring, the Agekeeper rolls the Cosmic Age Die to set the Sign, Planet, and Element of the Age. Each player rolls their Zodiac Die and places it on the matching Sign on the Zodiac Wheel — your Sign for the rest of the Age.
- **Your Tableau** — Everything you have in play, arranged in three zones. Spread — Minor Arcana face-up; effects active; cards craft Coal, pay Attunements, and score at Harvest. Hand — Minor Arcana held privately; safe from Duels; committed in Sieges. Arcanum — Major Arcana (Fates and Adepts); hold up to two Adept Cards.
- **Coal** — Wooden discs in your player color. Spend 3 cards of one suit to craft 1 Coal. Place Coal in a Furnace to raise your Heat in that Element.
- **Furnaces** — Four shared stations on the board, one per Element. Each Age you may fuel the Furnace matching your Sign's Element. Each Furnace holds three Coal total, shared among all players. Your Coal there raises your Heat by +1.
- **Adept Cards** — Major Arcana purchased for 3 Essence of any type when dealt. They live in your Arcanum and provide ongoing effects while in play.
- **Astral Houses & Mansions** — Spend 3 Spread cards whose Planets match your Sign's ruling Planet to build a House on your Sign — a permanent Harvest source. Upgrade to a Mansion with 2 more Planet cards plus a Reagent marker from the supply; a Mansion doubles its Alignment score.

### Step 4: Building Your Engine

The Great Work runs on two currencies: **Essence** and **Kismeta cards**. Each Spring you draw cards and grow your Tableau. Each Autumn you Harvest Essence. Spend cards to craft Coal and build Houses; spend Essence to Distill Reagents. All of it feeds the moment you Fire a Crucible Formula.

The beats below follow Essence and cards from Harvest through your Tableau, until you are ready to Fire.

| 🌾 Harvest Essence | ⚗️ Distill Reagents | 🏰 Craft & Build | 🃏 Grow Your Tableau |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *Essence from Alignment* | *Essence into Power* | *Cards into Coal and Houses* | *Spread, Hand & Arcanum* |
| Each Autumn, Align your Zodiac Die, Spread, and Astral Houses with the Cosmic Age to Harvest Essence. The Gift of the Age and Perfect Matches pay out the most. | Spend 5 Essence of one Element to Distill a Reagent of that Element. Reagents fuel Attunements and Crucible Formulas when you Fire your Stone. | Spend 3 cards of one suit to craft Coal. Spend 3 cards of one Planet to build an Astral House. Upgrade to a Mansion with 2 more Planet cards plus a Reagent from the supply. | Cards flow into your Spread each Spring and Summer. Your Hand is your Siege reserve; your Arcanum holds Adepts. |

### Step 5: The Transmutation Process

You will have four Crucible Cards in front of you. Each one moves your Philosopher's Stone one stage through the Forge: Lead, then Bronze, then Silver, and finally Gold.

When you are ready to Fire, complete the Alchemical Formula on the card. Turn in a card set, the required Reagents, and Coal in each Furnace.

Each card below is one beat in the Transmutation Process.

| 🔥 Fire the Stone | ⚔️ Defend Against Siege | ⏳ Temper the Stone | ❄️ Rekindle the Stone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *Attune & Enter the Forge* | *One Summer in the Forge* | *Advance the Great Work* | *Recover from Seizure* |
| Assemble the Attunement on a Crucible Card from your Spread, pay its Reagents and Furnace Heat, and commit up to 3 Shield Reagents. Your Stone leaves the Mantle Ring and enters the Forge. | Your Stone is exposed to Siege for the Summer it spends in the Forge. Rivals must match your Shield Reagents as an Ante to attack. The more you commit, the harder you are to Siege. | After a full Cosmic Age in the Forge, Temper your Stone to the next Mantle Ring position. Set the spent Crucible Card aside and consume the Coal used for that Stage. | If a rival Seizes your Stone in the Forge, pay 5 Essence of any type to Rekindle it. Your Stone returns to the Mantle Ring so you can Fire again when ready. |

### Step 6: Let the Great Work Begin

You have learned the path. Now it is time to set up the game and play.

Each Cosmic Age needs an **Agekeeper**. The Agekeeper sets the Cosmic Age each Spring, deals Kismeta cards, manages the deck, and resets the board at Transit.

## Part 3 — Your First Cosmic Age

### Step 14: Spring: Set the Cosmic Age

*Spring · 1 of 5*

Spring ushers in a new Cosmic Age. The Agekeeper rolls the Cosmic Age Die and announces the Sign, Planet, and Element that govern this Age. Every player takes the Gift of the Age: 1 Essence matching the Cosmic Age's Element.

- [ ] Agekeeper rolls the Cosmic Age Die onto the matching Sign
- [ ] Read the Sign, Planet, and Element aloud
- [ ] All players take 1 Essence of the Cosmic Age's Element
- [ ] Resolve any Fateful Wagers from the previous Winter

### Step 15: Spring: Set Your Zodiac Sign

*Spring · 2 of 5*

Every player rolls their Zodiac Die and places their Meeple on the rolled Sign. Your Sign sets which Furnace you may fuel, where you may build an Astral House, and how you Align at Harvest and in Sieges for the rest of the Age.

- [ ] All players roll their Zodiac Die
- [ ] Place Meeples on rolled Signs on the Zodiac Wheel

### Step 16: Spring: Deal the Kismeta Cards

*Spring · 3 of 5*

The Agekeeper deals **3 Kismeta cards face-down** to each player. Fate Cards resolve immediately when drawn; Adept Cards may be purchased for **3 Essence, of any type**, or discarded.

- [ ] Agekeeper deals 3 cards per player, face-down
- [ ] Resolve any Fate Cards drawn during the deal

### Step 17: Spring: Build Your Tableau

*Spring · 4 of 5*

Your **Tableau** is everything you have in play, arranged across three zones.

**Spread** — Minor Arcana played face-up. Effects are active; cards craft Coal, pay Attunements, and score at the Harvest. They can be lost in Duels.

**Hand** — Minor Arcana held privately. Effects are inactive; Hand cards are safe from Duels and may be committed in Sieges.

**Arcanum** — Major Arcana (Fates and Adepts). You may hold up to two Adept Cards.

- [ ] Arrange cards across Spread, Hand, and Arcanum
- [ ] Consider your goals for this Age

### Step 18: Spring: Card Lock

*Spring · 5 of 5*

Once your Tableau is arranged, cards are locked between Hand and Spread until Winter. New Minor Arcana gained in Summer or Autumn go to your Spread automatically.

📌 *Tip: Your Spread is your engine — visible and active. Your Hand is your reserve for Sieges.*

### Step 19: Summer: Craft, Distill, Trade & Contest

*Summer · 1 of 1*

On your turn, take any Summer actions in any order: craft Houses or Mansions, Distill Reagents, trade with rivals, Duel for cards, Extinguish Coal, or Siege an exposed Stone. Pass when done.

On your turn (clockwise from Agekeeper), take any actions below in any order, as many times as you like. Pass when you are done.

**Your turn**
**Craft & Distill**
- **Craft Astral House** *(optional)*
  - Spend 3 Spread cards whose Planets match your Sign's ruling Planet. Build the House on your Sign. Each House is a permanent Harvest Source.
- **Upgrade to Mansion** *(optional)*
  - Spend 2 more cards of that Sign's ruling Planet and place a Reagent from the supply beneath the House token as a marker (not spent from your stock).
- **Distill a Reagent** *(optional)*
  - Spend 5 Essence of one Element to take 1 Reagent of that Element from the supply.
**Contest Your Rivals**
- **Trade** *(optional)*
  - Freely exchange Adept Cards, Astral Houses, Essence, Kismeta Cards, and Reagents with any rival on your turn.
- **Duel** *(optional)*
  - Challenge a rival to a Zodiac Die roll. Highest roll wins (defender wins ties). Winner takes a card from the loser's Spread.
- **Extinguish Coal** *(optional)*
  - Use a card effect to remove a rival's Coal from a Furnace, dropping their Heat by 1.
- **Siege the Forge** *(optional)*
  - Attack a rival's Stone while it sits exposed in the Forge. Pay an Ante of Reagents equal to the Shield Reagents guarding that Stone.

### Step 20: Autumn: Harvest Elemental Essence

*Autumn · 1 of 2*

Autumn opens with **Harvest**. Each player compares their Harvest Sources — Zodiac Die, Astral Houses, Adept Cards, and Spread Cards — to the Cosmic Age's three Aspects: Sign, Planet, and Element.

Each matching Aspect scores Essence from the bank in the Cosmic Age's Element: Sign +3 · Planet +2 · Element +1. Stack every tier a source matches; match all three on one source for a **Perfect Match (+6)**.

Compare each Harvest Source to the Cosmic Age's Sign, Planet, and Element. Stack every tier a source matches; take your total in Essence of the Cosmic Age's Element.

### Step 21: Autumn: Fuel & Conduct the Great Work

*Autumn · 2 of 2*

After everyone Harvests, take your Crucible turn. If your Stone was Seized, Rekindle it first — then craft Coal to fuel Furnaces, Fire or Temper your Stone, and Distill Reagents if you need them before Firing.

📌 *Tip: When you Fire, commit up to 3 Reagents as Shield Reagents — rivals must match that number as an Ante to Siege you.*

After all players finish Harvest, take any Crucible actions below in any order on your turn. Tap an action for a quick reminder.

**Your turn**
**Crucible Actions**
- **Rekindle the Stone** *(optional)*
  - *When:* Your Stone is in Stasis
  - From the Autumn of the Age after your Stone was Seized, pay 5 Essence, of any type, to return it to its previous Forge position. If that station is occupied, wait until it is free.
- **Fuel Your Furnaces**
  - *When:* Craft Coal and place it in the Furnace matching your Sign's Element
  - Spend 3 cards of one suit to craft 1 Coal. Place your Coal in the Furnace matching your Sign's Element. Each Coal you own there raises your Heat by +1.
- **Distill a Reagent** *(optional)*
  - *When:* Available on your turn — same rules as Summer
  - Spend 5 Essence of one Element to take 1 Reagent of that Element. Useful before you Fire or set Shield Reagents.
- **Fire the Stone**
  - *When:* Stone on the Mantle Ring — complete an Alchemical Formula
  - Pay Attunement, Reagents, and Heat from an unfired Crucible Card. Move your Stone into the Forge. Commit up to 3 Reagents as Shield Reagents beside your Stone.
- **Temper the Stone**
  - *When:* Stone has remained in the Forge for one full Age
  - Advance your Stone to the next Mantle Ring position. Set the completed Formula aside. Consume the Coal used, lowering your Heat.

### Step 22: Winter: Final Actions & Reset

*Winter · 1 of 1*

Winter closes the Cosmic Age. Unlock cards between Hand and Spread, optionally place a Fateful Wager, enforce limits (Spread 7 · Hand 5 · Arcanum 2 Adepts), then Transit: shuffle the discard pile into the Kismeta Deck and pass the Key clockwise.

Complete Winter actions below in any order. Tap an action for a quick reminder.

**Your turn**
**Winter actions**
- **1. Card Unlock**
  - Cards are now unlocked. Move cards freely between your Hand and Spread before taking other Winter actions.
- **2. Fateful Wager (optional)**
  - Bet Cards and Reagents on the next Cosmic Age's Zodiac Sign. Guess correctly and your wager is doubled. Guess wrong and it is lost. Wagers resolve at the start of next Spring.
- **3. Enforce Card Limits**
  - Discard down to card limits before the next Age: Spread 7 cards · Hand 5 cards · Arcanum 2 Adept cards. Return Fate Cards to the discard pile at Transit.
- **4. Transit the Age**
  - Return Fate Cards to the discard pile. Clear Zodiac Dice and Meeples. The Agekeeper shuffles the discard pile into the Kismeta Deck, then passes the Key clockwise. Return to Spring.

### Step 23: Transit the Age: End of the Round

The Agekeeper passes the Key clockwise. A new Cosmic Age begins with Spring unless someone has completed the Great Work.

- [ ] Shuffle the Kismeta discard pile back into the deck
- [ ] Pass the Agekeeper Key to the next player
- [ ] Start the next Cosmic Age with Spring, or check for a winner
