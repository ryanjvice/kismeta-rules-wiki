# KISMETA: The Veiled Ascent

> *A Fast-paced Card Game of Calculated Risks & Energetic Shifts*

---

## Objective

Ascend through your seven chakras to gain enlightenment before you are corrupted by the chaotic energy of the Aethereal Veil. Each round, build a three-card spread and try to outscore the Table's hand without going over the **50-point threshold**. Victories move you upward on your ascension path while defeats add to your corruption level.

---

## Setup

**Table:**
- 1 Tarot Deck
- The Veil Progress Board

**Each Player Gets:**
- 1 Player Token (Ascension Marker)
- 1 Corruption Token

---

## Gameplay Steps

*Gameplay is the same regardless of the number of players.*

### Step 1: Deal the Veil *(Dealer/AI)*

Deal 3 cards for the Table:
- First deal **1 face-up**
- Then deal **2 face-down**
- These cards stay hidden until Step 4.

---

### Step 2: Build Your Spread *(Player)*

Draw 4 cards:
- First deal **1 face-down** — this card stays hidden until Step 4
- Then deal **3 face-up**

Review your revealed cards. You will be adding the value of your unrevealed card in the next step.

**Discard 1 face-up card.**

> **Your final Spread = 2 face-up cards + your face-down card.**

---

### Step 3: Reveal & Resolve

**Say aloud:** *"The Veil is lifted."*

1. **Reveal** all face-down cards (Table's and yours)
2. **Check** for 3-card patterns in your Spread (announce if you have one)
3. **Calculate** each side's total (do not exceed 50)
4. **Apply** pattern effects and determine outcome
5. **Move** tokens and adjust Corruption accordingly

## Card Values & Scoring

### Minor Arcana (Numbered Cards)

| Card | Value |
|------|-------|
| Ace | 1 or 11 points |
| 2–10 | Face value |
| Page | 11 points |
| Knight | 12 points |
| Queen | 13 points |
| King | 14 points |

### Major Arcana (Special Cards)

Major Arcana cards give you a choice — use the card's **base value** (the Roman numeral shown):

| Card | Value |
|------|-------|
| The Magician (I) | 1 |
| The High Priestess (II) | 2 |
| The Lovers (VI) | 6 |
| The World (XXI) | 21 |

## Round Outcomes

| Outcome | Result |
|---------|--------|
| Your total > Table | Advance 1 space |
| Exact 50 | Advance 2 spaces |
| Table busts (you don't) | Advance 1 space & remove 1 Corruption |
| Tie | No movement or penalty |
| Loss (Table wins or you Overreach) | Move to Corruption Zone, +1 Corruption |
| Lose again in Corruption Zone | Stay in place, +1 Corruption |

> **Note:** Pattern bonuses may modify these outcomes — see [Pattern Bonuses](#pattern-bonuses) below.

---

## Veil Progression Board

| Space Type | Meaning |
|------------|---------|
| **Chakras** *(center)* | Core milestones. Some reward Aether Tokens, others remove Corruption Marks. |
| **Ascension Zones** *(gradient)* | You must pass through these between Chakras. |
| **Corruption Zones** *(dark)* | Landing here adds 1 Corruption Mark. You remain until you win again. |

- Players **never fall below** their highest Chakra reached.
- If you reach **6 Corruption**, you are **overtaken by the Veil**.

---

## Multiplayer Rules

- One Tarot deck per **2 players**.
- One player acts as the **dealer**, resolving the Table hand.
- All players **share** the Table's hand each round but resolve their Spreads individually.

---

## Winning the Game

Reach the **Crown Chakra** before gaining 6 Corruption.

That's it. No score, no tricks — just survival through resonance.

---

## Pattern Bonuses

When your final 3-card Spread forms certain patterns, you unlock powerful bonuses.

> **Only ONE pattern bonus applies per round.**

### 3-Card Flush
*3 cards in the same Suit (any Rank)*
**Example:** 5, 8, & Page of Wands

**Bonus: Add +3 to your total**

---

### Straight
*3 cards in sequential Rank (any Suit)*
**Example:** Ace–2–3

**Bonus: Add +5 to your total**

---

### Three of a Kind
*3 cards with the same Rank (any Suit)*
**Example:** Queen–Queen–Queen

**Bonus: Add +7 to your total**

---

### Straight Flush
*Both a Straight AND a Flush*
**Example:** 4–5–6 of Swords

**Bonus: Add +9 to your total**

---

### Royal Flush
*A Straight Flush with Court Cards (Page, Knight, Queen, King)*
**Example:** Knight–Queen–King of Pentacles

**Bonus: Add +11 to your total**

## GLOSSARY OF TERMS

| TERM | DEFINITION |
|------|-----------|
| **Aethereal Veil** | The chaotic, shifting energy that surrounds the players. If you reach 6 Corruption Marks, the Veil has overtaken you and you lose. |
| **Ascension Zone** | Gradient spaces between Chakras on the Veil Progression Board. Players pass through these on the way to each Chakra milestone. |
| **Aether Token** | A reward token gained by reaching certain Chakra milestones. Used to remove Corruption Marks or as indicated by the board. |
| **Chakra** | A core milestone space on the Veil Progression Board. Reaching a Chakra may grant rewards (Aether Tokens or Corruption removal). Players never fall below their highest Chakra reached. |
| **Corruption** | The measure of how much the Aethereal Veil is consuming you. Each Corruption Mark brings you closer to defeat. Reaching 6 Corruption Marks ends your game. |
| **Corruption Mark** | A token placed on your Corruption Track when you lose a round or land in a Corruption Zone. |
| **Corruption Zone** | Dark spaces on the Veil Progression Board. Landing here adds 1 Corruption Mark. You remain in the Corruption Zone until you win a round. |
| **Crown Chakra** | The seventh and final Chakra milestone at the top of the Veil Progression Board. Reaching it wins the game. |
| **Dealer** | The player (or AI in solo play) who deals the Table's hand each round. In multiplayer, one player acts as Dealer and resolves the Table hand. |
| **Major Arcana** | The 22 special (trump) cards in a Tarot deck. Each carries a Roman numeral that can be used as its point value. |
| **Minor Arcana** | The 56 numbered suit cards in a Tarot deck (Wands, Cups, Swords, Pentacles). Point values match face value, with court cards worth 11–14. |
| **Overreach** | Going over the 50-point threshold with your Spread total, resulting in an automatic loss for that round. |
| **Pattern Bonus** | A scoring bonus applied when your 3-card Spread forms a qualifying pattern: Flush, Straight, Three of a Kind, Straight Flush, or Royal Flush. Only one bonus applies per round. |
| **Spread** | Your final 3-card hand: 2 face-up cards plus your hidden face-down card. Revealed during Reveal & Resolve. |
| **The Table** | The dealer's 3-card hand (1 face-up, 2 face-down) that all players must try to outscore without Overreaching. |
