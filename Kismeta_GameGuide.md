# KISMETA

Kismeta: Alchemists of the Great Year is a strategic card-and-dice game where you and up to three friends take on the roles of rival Alchemists. Your quest is to complete the Great Work, transforming your Philosopher's Stone from Lead into Gold. Travel through the Cosmic Ages of the Great Year, pursuing this transformation under the watchful gaze of the Fates, the eternal guardians of the Crucible of Kismeta.

**Players:** 2–4

**Play Time:** 1st Play/Quickplay ~ _1–2 hrs._ | Standard ~ _2–3 hrs._ | Magnus Alchemist ~ _3+ hrs._

# LORE: ALCHEMISTS OF THE GREAT YEAR

Long ago, alchemists sought to uncover the divine mysteries of the Cosmos, whispering secrets to the Fates and bending material existence to their will. Within the fiery Crucible of Kismeta, they transformed scarcity into abundance, ushering in an era of incredible prosperity. Their lives were graced with wealth, wonder, and limitless potential, ushering in a golden age for humanity.

At the heart of their miraculous pursuits lay the Philosopher's Stone, the ultimate symbol of cosmic perfection and the key to immortality. Alchemists toiled over their crucibles and cauldrons for ages, each seeking to claim the Stone's elusive power as their own. Yet despite their brilliance and devotion, the Stone remained just beyond humanity's reach.

The Fates fell silent, and the former Golden Age faded into distant history as the Great Year continued its cycle. Cosmic energy within the solar system waned, drawing consciousness into darkness. This marked the start of a long, treacherous Dark Age. Discussions of the Alchemist's Great Work and the quest for the Philosopher's Stone became myths and folklore.

But now the Fates whisper to humanity once more. Their voices echo across the ages, summoning the brightest minds to a single place: the hallowed Nexus, a sacred cosmic convergence outside of time, where destinies are forged, and rivalries burn bright. Across the Cosmic Ages, the Fates have tasked you once again with the Great Work of those long-forgotten Alchemists of old.

The first among you to complete the Great Work, reaching the Altar of Kismeta with your golden Philosopher's Stone intact, will rise as the Magnus Alchemista Grandis Illuminatus ex Kismeta, the prophesied Alchemist destined to lead humanity into a new Golden Age.

The Fates have beckoned you, dear Alchemist.

The Crucible of Kismeta awaits.

# GAME OVERVIEW

## The Great Year

Each game of Kismeta: Alchemists of the Great Year takes you on a journey through a single Great Year, the grand cycle of the solar system spiraling around the galaxy's core. You experience each Cosmic Age, represented by a Zodiac sign chosen at random at the start of each round by the Agekeeper's dice. These signs hold unique Aspects and Effects that shape resource flow and interactions throughout the cycle. You'll roll your Zodiac dice to determine your own sign for the Age, receiving that Sign's Cosmic Effect as your personal blessing for the round, and work to align your Aspects with the Cosmic Age, gathering valuable Kismeta Cards.

The Cosmic Age unfolds through four Seasons, each offering distinct opportunities to advance your Great Work: Spring sets the Age and gathers resources; Summer is for trading, dueling, and preparing; Autumn fires Stones into the Forge to push your progress; and Winter marks the end, resetting the board for the next cycle. As you traverse these Ages, focus on maximizing your Alignments, managing resources, enduring the Crucible's heat, and outlasting your rivals.

## The Great Work

This is your driving objective: to be the first to transform lead into the legendary Philosopher's Stone. This pursuit guides all your seasonal actions. Cards from the Harvest become Reagents, which you use to complete Crucible Cards, guiding your Stone through each Stage of Transmutation in the Forge.

Keep your Stone protected in the Forge, for opposing Alchemists may challenge you with Opposition tests. If they succeed, your Stone enters Stasis, pausing your progress until you can resume forging.

## The Altar of Kismeta

Ultimately, the first Alchemist to complete all four Crucible Cards and carry their Stone through every Stage of Transformation to the Altar of Kismeta will finish the Great Work and claim victory.

All Hail! All Hail!
The Magnus Alchemista Grandis Illuminatus ex Kismeta!

# SETUP

## I. Set the Table

- Place the Great Year Board game board in the center of the play area.
- Select a Game Mode before continuing. _(Next page)_
- Sort the Kismeta cards into two piles, Kismeta Cards (Blue) and Crucible Cards (Red).
- Common Deck (Blue): Shuffle all cards and place them face down by the game board.
- Crucible Deck (Red): Place next to the Great Year board. You'll build the Deck in step _IV. Build the Crucible Deck._
- Set the following components within easy reach:
  - 1 Agekeeper's Key: 1 black pawn game piece; _(or a magical trinket from your home.)_
  - 1 Cosmic Age Dice: (12-sided Die, black)
  - Reagents: Sort by color and keep nearby.

## II. Become an Alchemist

Choose a color (Red, Green, Blue, or White) and take the following matching gear:

- **4 Astral House Tokens:** Tokens with 🏰 symbol; Keep these nearby.
- **1 Cauldron Codex:** Dual-sided card; Cauldron Codex & Quick Reference Guide on reverse.
- **4 Molten Coals:** Small black crystal pieces; Keep these nearby.
- **1 Meeple:** Wooden game piece; Keep this nearby.
- **1 Philosopher's Stone:** Place this on the "START" space in your section of the Mantle Ring.
- **1 Zodiac Die:** 12-sided Die, matching your chosen color; Keep this nearby.

## III. Select a Game Mode

Choose your Game Mode before continuing. Each Mode adjusts the starting conditions and the game's economy. The round structure, phases, and rules remain as outlined in this guide for all modes.
Look for the "⚙️" icon in the Game Guide, highlighting modifiers. Continue to _Build The Crucible Deck._

### 1️⃣ QUICKPLAY / FIRST PLAY

_Recommended for first playthroughs._

- **Crucible Deck:** Use the Quickplay deck build.
- **Astral Houses:** Built by paying 1 Kismeta Card whose Planet matches your current Zodiac Sign.
- **Cauldron Thresholds:** Each Molten Coal placed in any Cauldron reduces the Crafting cost for all Reagents BY 5 RP. The Base RP Threshold to start is 25 RP.

### 2️⃣ STANDARD GAME

_Recommended once you are familiar with the game rules & game flow._

- Play the game as written throughout this guide.
- Ignore all ⚙️ Game Mode Modifiers.
- Use the Standard deck build.

### 3️⃣ MAGNUS ALCHEMIST (MASTERY)

_For those returning from previous Great Years, seeking an even greater challenge._

- All rules from the Standard mode apply.
- Use the Magnus Alchemist deck build.
- Alignments between Players influence all Duels, Gambits & Oppositions.

#### Player to Player Alignments:

Players' Zodiac Sign Alignments affect their Trading, Dueling, Gambits, and Oppositions.

- **Aligned:** Players' Zodiac Signs share at least 1 Aspect in common
- **Misaligned:** Players' Zodiac Signs do not share any Aspects
- **Trades:** Aligned players trade freely. Misaligned players must trade 2:1 in favor of the non-initiating player.
- **Duels, Gambits & Oppositions:** Challengers gain +1 to their dice roll (Duels & Gambits) or Alignment Points (Oppositions) when confronting a Misaligned opponent. Misalignment is checked at the start of the challenge using each player's current Zodiac Die result, before the Opponent Age is set.
- Aligned players have no additional bonus in Duels, Gambits, or Oppositions.

## V. Determine the First Agekeeper

The Agekeeper is the curator of the round. You hold the Agekeeper's Key and are responsible for:

- **Setting the Cosmic Age:** You roll the Cosmic Age Die to start the round.
- **Managing the Kismeta Card Deck:** You deal the Harvest cards to all players and manage the discard pile.
- **Facilitate Autumn Turns:** Beginning with yourself, guide each player through their Autumn turn in clockwise order. Each player surveys the Crucible, then resolves Opposition, Crucible Actions (Temper and/or Fire the Stone), and Leave Stasis in that sequence.
- **Transit the Age:** Prepare the play area for the next Age. Pass the Agekeeper's Key clockwise at the end of every round.

Who starts? Everyone rolls their dice; the highest roller becomes the first Agekeeper and takes the Agekeeper's Key first. Reroll if tied.

The Agekeeper's Key is passed clockwise at the end of each round.

## VI. Deal the Crucible Cards

The first Agekeeper prepares the Crucible Cards before the game begins.

- **Build the Crucible Deck:** (If you haven't yet done so) Assemble the Crucible Deck according to player count and chosen difficulty.

\*See **Building The Crucible Deck\***

- **Deal the Crucible Cards:**
  - Shuffle the Crucible Deck. Tap it twice to cleanse it to usher in the new Great Year.
  - Deal four Crucible Cards face down to each player.
  - Each player places their four Crucible Cards face-down in a single row along their edge of the Great Year board.

- **Lay the Molten Coals:**
  - Take your four Molten Coals and place one atop each of your Crucible Cards.
  - The cards are now Dormant, their Formulae hidden.

📌 **Note:** The position of each card (I, II, III, & IV; from left to right) corresponds to a Formula shown on your Cauldron Codex.

- **Deal Starting Kismeta Cards:** The Agekeeper deals one face-up card from the Common Deck to each player's Spread. If any player receives a Major Arcana, return it to the deck, reshuffle, tap twice to cleanse, and redeal until all players hold a Minor Arcana.

- The first Cosmic Age begins!

## PHASE 1: 🌸 SPRING

### Set the Age & Gather Resources

Spring ushers in a new Cosmic Age. The Agekeeper rolls the Cosmic Age Die. All players determine their Zodiac Sign, and the Harvest fills everyone's hand for the upcoming round. Manage your inventory wisely, and what you sow in the Spring may well bear fruit in seasons yet to come.

| 🌸 SPRING Set the Age & Gather Resources | 1️⃣ Set the Cosmic Age: Agekeeper rolls the Cosmic Age Die: Read the Sign & Aspects aloud. 2️⃣ Determine Your Sign: All players roll their Zodiac Die. Move their Meeple to their Sign. 3️⃣ Harvest: Base Harvest + Bonus Cards + Agekeeper's Boon (when applicable). 4️⃣ Commune: Build your Spread, Hand, and Arcanum zones. Consider your goals. 5️⃣ Card Lock: Cards are locked between your Hand and Spread until Phase 4: Winter. |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

### 1️⃣ Set the Cosmic Age

- **Roll the Cosmic Age Die:** The Agekeeper rolls the 12-sided black die.
- **Read the Sign and Aspects aloud:** Announce the Sign's name, its ruling Planet, and its Element. All players note these — every Alignment calculation this round is measured against them.
- **Apply the Cosmic Effect:** The rolled Sign's Cosmic Effect activates immediately and applies for the entire round. See the _Cosmic Ages & Effects_ table in the Reference Appendix.

### 2️⃣ Determine Your Sign

- **Roll your Zodiac Die:** Each player rolls their color-matched 12-sided die simultaneously.
- **Move your Meeple:** Advance it to the Sign you rolled on the Zodiac Wheel. Your Sign is your cosmic identity for this Age. It shapes your Alignments and grants you a personal Cosmic Effect.

Each player receives the Cosmic Effect listed for their own rolled Sign, active throughout the round. This is in addition to the shared Cosmic Age Effect, which applies to all players. If your Sign matches the current Cosmic Age, its Cosmic Effect is simply active for you — it does not stack or apply twice.

📌 **Note:** Your Sign's Aspects (Sign, Planet, Element) also serve as an Alignment source for this round's Harvest and all other interactions. Both your personal Cosmic Effect and your Alignment contributions remain active until Winter Reset.

### 3️⃣ Harvest Kismeta Cards

All players earn a base of 2 Kismeta Cards at the start of every Harvest. You can earn additional Bonus Cards by aligning items from your inventory with the Cosmic Age's Aspects. Each player calculates their own total; the Agekeeper confirms and deals the full Harvest.

#### Harvest Sources

- **Zodiac Die** — Compare your rolled Sign's Aspects (Sign, Planet, Element) against the Cosmic Age. Earn the bonus for the highest match.
- **Astral Houses** — Each Astral House you've built is checked independently. Multiple Astral Houses each earn their own bonus and stack with all other sources.
- **Specialist Cards** — Each active Specialist Card in your Arcanum carries three Aspects. Each one is scored independently as a Harvest Source.
- **Spread Element Match** — Before the Harvest deal, count the cards already in your Spread whose suit matches the Cosmic Age's Element. Each matching card earns +1 card.

⚠️ **Important — Highest Aspect Only Per Source:** Each source scores its single highest-matching Aspect. If your Zodiac Die result matches both the Sign and the Planet of the Cosmic Age, the score is **+3**, not +5.

⚠️ **If the Common Deck runs out during Harvest:** Immediately reshuffle the discard pile to form a new deck and continue dealing. If no discard pile is available, the Fates intervene: all players discard their entire Hand to the Common Deck, which is then reshuffled, and the Harvest continues from there.

#### Aspects & Alignment

Each Cosmic Age has three Aspects — **Sign**, **Planet**, and **Element** — that define which cards, dice results, and structures Align with it. The closer your Alignment, the more cards you earn in the Harvest. Every Harvest Bonus source in your possession is scored against the current Cosmic Age's three Aspects. Each source earns bonus cards based on its highest matching Aspect.

### 4️⃣ Commune with the Kismeta Cards

Now that you have your Kismeta Cards for the Age, it's time to commune with them. Cards you place in your Spread will have active effects that influence gameplay in many ways. Spread cards can be used to complete card sets for your Cauldron Codex and Crucible cards' Alchemical Formulas, or to craft Reagents. They are vulnerable to being lost in duels and serve other functions in the game.

The cards you hold privately are your Hand cards. They can be used to craft Reagents and in Oppositions, but do not have active effects and cannot be used to complete card sets. They cannot be lost in duels.

Any Major Arcana card (Adept Card or Fate Card) is played into a separate zone, the Arcanum.

📌 **Note:** Your Spread is your engine — cards here count toward Alignment, Crucible activation, and Crafting. Your Hand is your reserve, useful for Oppositions and Crafting but invisible to rivals.

### 5️⃣ Card Lock 🔒

Once the Harvest is complete, all cards are locked in their current zones until the beginning of **Phase 4: Winter**. Any new cards you gain in Summer or Autumn are automatically added to your Spread zone.

📌 **Spring is complete.** The Cosmic Age has been set, resources gathered, and your inventory built. Proceed to **Phase 2: ☀️ Summer**.

## PHASE 2: ☀️ SUMMER

### Trade, Build & Prepare

The Sun shines brightly as you and your fellow Alchemists focus on your plans. Summer is the perfect time for socializing; bartering over trades, initiating duels, and risking your luck in gambits. While fortune favors the bold, Fate may have other plans in store for you. In clockwise order, beginning with the Agekeeper, each player takes their turn and can perform any of the listed actions multiple times and in any sequence.

| ☀️ SUMMER Trade, Build & Prepare | **CRAFT RESOURCES (TAKE ANY ACTION)** 1. Activate Crucible Card 2. Build Astral House 3. Craft Reagent **CONSORT WITH RIVALS (TAKE ANY ACTION)** 4. Trade 5. Duel 6. Gambit |
| -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

### 1️⃣ Activate Crucible Cards

Your Cauldron Codex holds four Formulae - one per card position (I–IV). Activate each card by collecting and discarding the matching card set. You may activate any number in any order, and may do so in Phase 2: Summer or Phase 3: Autumn.

#### How to Activate:

- **Consult your Codex:** Follow the Codex for formulas of card sets you'll need to collect.
- **Assemble the card set:** Collect the required cards in your Spread.
- **Discard the card set:** Remove all cards in the set from your Spread.
- **Drop the Molten Coal:** Move the Coal from the Crucible Card into any Cauldron of your choice.
- **Activate the Crucible Card:** Flip the card face-up to Activate it. Each Crucible card reveals an Alchemical Formula that is necessary to advance your Philosopher's Stone one step closer to the Altar of Kismeta.

⚠️ **Important:** You may only have one Crucible Card Fired at a time. If your Stone is already Forging, you must Temper it before Firing again.

### 2️⃣ Build Astral Houses

Astral Houses are permanent structures built on the Zodiac Wheel. Each provides three ongoing benefits and cannot be moved once placed. Only one player may build on any given Sign - but other players may still land on or Align with that Sign.

#### How to Build Astral Houses:

- Pay 2 Kismeta Cards matching your current Sign's Planet.
- Place your 🏰 token on the 🏰 icon on that Sign.

| 🏰 ASTRAL HOUSES          |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **BENEFIT**               | **HOW IT WORKS**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Harvest Bonus             | Acts as an Alignment source each Spring. Earns +3 (Sign match), +2 (Planet match), or +1 (Element match) against the Cosmic Age. Stacks with all other Harvest sources.                                                                           |
| Opposition Alignment      | Contributes Alignment Points in Oppositions as both Challenger and Defender: +3 (Sign), +2 (Planet), +1 (Element) against the Opposition Age.                                                                                                     |
| Cosmic Effect (Permanent) | Permanently unlocks that Sign's Cosmic Effect for you every round, regardless of your Zodiac Die result. If you also roll that Sign, the effect is simply active — it does not apply twice. Multiple Houses each grant their own unlocked effect. |

_⚙️ **Game Mode Modifier (Quickplay):** Cost reduced to 1 card matching the Sign's Planet._

### 3️⃣ Craft Reagents

Reagents are the fuels needed to Fire your Stone. You can craft them using cards from your Spread or Hand. The color of the card's Suit matches the color of the Reagent you can craft with it. Add up the ranks of the cards you collect, then compare the total to your current Reagent RP Threshold. If the total meets or exceeds the RP Threshold, you can trade the cards in to take a Reagent from the supply.

The starting Base RP Threshold is 30 RP in Standard & Magnus Alchemist Mode, and 25 RP in Quickplay. You can reduce the RP Threshold for crafting Reagents by activating your Crucible cards and moving the Molten Coal from the Crucible Card into one of your four Crucible Cauldrons. Each Molten Coal placed in a Crucible Cauldron reduces the cost to craft a Reagent by 5 RP.

#### How to Craft:

- **Choose a Reagent type:** Select which of the four Reagents you want to Craft. See Correspondence Table.
- **Gather cards:** Collect cards of the matching suit from your Spread and/or Hand.
- **Check the Threshold:** Confirm the combined RP meets or exceeds the current Threshold, based on the number of Molten Coals you have in your Cauldrons. The starting Base Threshold is 30 RP in the Standard and Magnus Alchemist Mode, and 25 RP in Quickplay.
- **Exchange:** Discard the cards and take 1 Reagent token from the supply.

💡 **Example:** Your Red Cauldron has 2 Coals (Threshold: 20 RP). You discard a 6 of Wands (6 RP) + 8 of Wands (8 RP) + Knight of Wands (11 RP) = 25 RP. Threshold met - take 1 Sulphur. (Wands → Fire → Red Cauldron → Sulphur.)

_⚙️ **Game Mode Modifier (Quickplay):** All Cauldrons share one simplified Threshold. Each Coal placed in any Cauldron reduces the cost for all Reagents regardless of color. Base RP is 25, going down to 5 RP with four Molten Coals._

Freely exchange resources with any rival during your turn.

📌 **Note:** Trading is the only way to transfer Reagents and Active Crucible Cards between players. They cannot be won in Duels or moved any other way.

_⚙️ **Game Mode Modifier (Magnus Alchemist):** Misaligned players must trade 2:1 in favor of the non-initiating player._

### 5️⃣ Duel

Challenge a rival to steal a single card from their Spread. Only the Challenger pays an entry fee; the Defender does not.

#### How to Declare a Duel:

- **Declare:** Name your opponent and the specific card you are targeting in their Spread.
- **Pay entry fee:** Choose an Ante Card from your Spread. You will lose this card if you lose the duel.
- **Check Spreads:** Both players review their Spread for any relevant card effects.
- **Roll:** Both players roll their Zodiac Dice. Higher roll wins. Reroll all ties until resolved.
- **Resolve:** See outcomes below.

| DUEL RESULTS     |                                                              |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **RESULT**       | **OUTCOME**                                                  |
| Challenger Wins  | Steal the card that was targeted from the Defender's Spread. |
| Challenger Loses | Return the Ante Card to the Common Deck.                     |

_⚙️ **Game Mode Modifier (Magnus Alchemist):** If Misaligned with your target, add +1 to your dice roll. The Defender receives no bonus._

### 6️⃣ Gambit

A Gambit lets you seize a rival's Active Crucible Card by proving you have already mastered its Alignment requirements - at the cost of one of your own. Gambits may only be declared during Phase 2: Summer, and only when your Spread fully satisfies the target card's complete Alignment requirement at the moment of declaration.

#### How to Instigate a Gambit:

- **Identify the target:** Point to the rival's Active Crucible Card your Spread satisfies. Demonstrate that your Spread meets its full Alignment requirement.
- **Name your offer:** Declare which of your own Active Crucible Cards you are putting up in exchange.
- **Pay the entry fee:** Pay the bank Reagents equal to the Defender's Ward Reagents on the targeted card. No Ward = no fee.
- **Roll:** Both players roll their Zodiac Dice. Apply any card effects that modify Gambits before comparing results.
- **Resolve:** See outcomes below.

| GAMBIT RESULTS   |                                                                                                                                                     |                             |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| **Result**       | **Challenger**                                                                                                                                      | **Defender**                |
| Challenger Wins  | The two Active Crucible Cards swap positions.                                                                                                       | Ward Reagents are spent.    |
| Challenger Loses | Offered card is Arrested (face-down, sideways; cannot Fire this round; resets to Active next round). Cannot re-Gambit the same Defender this round. | Ward Reagents are returned. |

#### Ward Reagents on Crucible Cards

During your turn in Phase 2, you may place Reagents directly on any Active Crucible Card as Ward Reagents. These set the entry fee that any Challenger must pay to Gambit for that card.

- Wards may not be placed reactively once a Gambit has been declared.
- No limit to the number of Reagents placed on a single card.
- Ward Reagents are spent if the Challenger wins; returned if the Challenger loses.
- Wards are permanent once placed and cannot be moved until the card is Fired.
- Ward Reagents CAN be used to satisfy the card's own Alchemical Formula requirements.

_⚙️ **Game Mode Modifier (Magnus Alchemist):** If Misaligned with the Defender at declaration, add +1 to the Challenger's dice roll. The Defender receives no bonus._

### 2️⃣ Opposition ⚔️

An Opposition is an Alignment contest against a rival whose Stone is currently Forging. If the Challenger wins, the Defender's Stone is sent to Stasis and must wait a round before retrying.

#### How to Declare an Opposition:

- **Pay the entry fee:** Pay the bank Reagents equal to the Defender's Ward Reagents. No Ward = no fee.
- **Cards in play:** All Spread cards participate automatically. You may also contribute Hand cards (discarded after use). All active Arcanum effects apply.
- **Roll:** Both players roll their Zodiac Dice and place them on their Sign.
- **Calculate Alignment Points:** Total all Aspect matches from every source in play. Highest score wins. On a tie, both players reroll; repeat until resolved.

#### Outcomes

| RESULT          | OUTCOME                                                                                                                                                                 |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Challenger Wins | Defender's Stone moves to the nearest ❄️ Stasis zone. Ward Reagents discarded. Crucible Card remains Fired. Challenger cannot challenge same Defender again this round. |
| Defender Wins   | Stone stays in the Forge. Challenger is Exhausted — cannot challenge the same Defender again this round.                                                                |

#### Multiple Oppositions Against the Same Stone

More than one player may challenge the same Forging Stone in the same round. Resolve each Opposition fully before the next is declared, in clockwise order from the previous Challenger.

📌 **Besieged Bonus:** Each time the Defender wins an Opposition this round, they earn +1 cumulative Alignment Points for all subsequent Oppositions that same round. Automatic — no action required.

#### Ward Reagents in the Forge

Once you place your Stone in the Forge, you may place Reagents next to it on the 🛡️ icon as Ward Reagents. These set the entry fee that any Challenger must pay to declare an Opposition against your Stone.

Ward Reagents placed in the Forge follow these rules:

1. **Lose in Opposition:** Wards are discarded to the supply.
2. **Win in Opposition:** Wards remain in place for the next round.
3. **Advance to Mantle Successfully:** Remaining Wards are burned and returned to the supply — not recovered.

_⚙️ **Game Mode Modifier (Magnus Alchemist):** If Misaligned with the Defender at declaration, add +1 to your final Alignment Points total. The Defender receives no bonus._

⚠️ **Important:** A Stone Fired into the Forge this Autumn cannot be targeted by Opposition this round. Only Stones that were already Forging at the start of this Autumn are eligible targets.

### 3️⃣ Crucible Actions

Your Stone's available actions depend on its current position. On your turn, take whichever of the following applies.

#### Temper the Stone ⏳

Available if your Stone's current position is on #1, 3, 5, or 7.

A Stone that has been Forging since the previous round and has survived all Oppositions this Autumn may now advance to the next stage.

- **Advance your Stone:** Move it from the Forge to the next Mantle Ring position.
- **Discard the Crucible Card:** The current Crucible Card is removed from play. The Stage is complete.
- **Burn remaining Wards:** Any Ward Reagents remaining on the 🛡️ symbol are returned to the supply. They are not recovered.

⚠️ **Important:** A Stone that returned from Stasis this round cannot Temper. It must complete a full round of Forging first.

#### Fire the Stone 🔥

Available if your Stone's current position is on #0, 2, 4, or 6.

If you have an Active Crucible Card whose Alchemical Formula you have satisfied, you may Fire your Stone into the Forge this Autumn.

#### How to Fire

- **Confirm Alignment:** Show your Spread satisfies the Crucible Card's Alignment requirement. Discard the cards.
- **Pay the Reagents:** Discard the required Reagents to the supply. These cannot be used as Ward Reagents for your card in the Forge.
- **Set your Ward Reagent:** Place Reagents on the 🛡️ symbol beside your Stone in the Forge. These set the entry fee that challengers must pay to declare an Opposition. No Ward = no entry fee (but you are a free target).
- **Turn the Crucible Card sideways:** It is now Fired.
- **Advance your Stone:** Move it from the Mantle Ring to the next Forge position.

#### Alchemical Formula

Each Crucible Card shows its Alchemical Formula; two requirements you must satisfy before Firing your Stone:

| CRUCIBLE CARDS: ALCHEMICAL FORMULAS |                                                                                                                                           |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **REQUIREMENT**                     | **WHAT IT REQUIRES**                                                                                                                      |
| Reagents                            | Specific Reagent types and quantities to discard when Firing, listed on the card.                                                         |
| Alignment                           | A precise card configuration in your Spread - specified by suit, planet, rank, or a combination. Discard the cards at the time of Firing. |

#### Ward Reagents in the Forge

Ward Reagents placed beside your Stone on the 🛡️ symbol follow different rules from Wards placed on Crucible Cards:

- **A. Lose in Opposition:** Wards are discarded to the supply.
- **B. Win in Opposition:** Wards remain in place for the next round.
- **C. Advance to Mantle Successfully:** Remaining Wards are burned and returned to the supply — not recovered.

### 4️⃣ Leave Stasis ❄️

Players whose Stone is currently in the Stasis zone may return it to the Forge this Autumn.

#### Steps to Leave Stasis

- **Move your Stone:** Return it to the Forge position it previously held.
- **Re-arm your Ward Reagent:** Place Reagents on the 🛡️ symbol beside your Stone (optional but recommended).
- **Resume Forging:** Your Crucible Card remains Fired. You must complete a full round in the Forge before you may Temper.

#### If Your Forge Position Is Occupied

If another player's Stone now holds your previous Forge position, choose one:

| OPTION                    | HOW IT WORKS                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Wait in Stasis            | Remain in Stasis another round. Attempt to return next Autumn. No action required.                                                                                            |
| Declare Stasis Opposition | Challenge the occupying Stone during Step 2: Opposition. Follows all standard Opposition rules, but with a different outcome (position swap, not send-to-Stasis). See Step 2. |

#### Stasis Opposition outcomes:

| RESULT           | OUTCOME                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Challenger Wins  | The two Stones swap positions. Challenger's Stone moves into the Forge; Defender's Stone is sent to the nearest Stasis zone. Defender's Wards discarded; Crucible Card remains Fired. |
| Challenger Loses | Challenger's Stone remains in Stasis for another round.                                                                                                                               |

📌 **Note:** All other Opposition rules apply to a Stasis Opposition: pay the Defender's Ward cost as the entry fee, Spread cards are active, Hand cards are optional, and card effects resolve before scores are compared.

# QUICK TIPS & STRATEGY

Keep these principles in mind as you find your footing across the first few rounds.

1. **Your Alignments Shape the Round:** Every Harvest bonus, Opposition advantage, and Gambit opportunity flows from the Aspect Alignments between the Cosmic Age and each of your inventory items.

2. **Your Spread Is Your Engine:** Cards in your Spread have active effects and count toward Alignment. Cards in your Hand are hidden and mostly dormant. Build your Spread deliberately each round — what's visible is what works.

3. **Cauldron Drops Are Permanent:** Each activated Crucible Card drops its Molten Coal into a Cauldron, permanently lowering that Cauldron's Crafting Threshold by 5 RP. Think ahead: fuel the Cauldron for the Reagent type you'll need most. (See Correspondence table.)

4. **Crafting Reagents from Your Hand:** Don't overlook your Hand when crafting Reagents. Cards in your Hand can be used alongside Spread cards to meet the Threshold. By crafting from your Hand, you keep the cards in your Spread active and ready to complete a card set from your Codex or an Alignment for a Crucible Card.

5. **Astral Houses Are Your Anchor:** Build Astral Houses early. Each House acts as a permanent Alignment source for Harvesting Bonus cards every Spring for the rest of the game. A House built in round one compounds its advantage over every subsequent Age. A House built in the final round is barely worth the cost.

6. **Adept Cards Synergies:** Your Arcanum has two Adept slots that, as a pair, permanently add their Sign, Planet, and Element to all calculations. A strong pair spans multiple Aspect types, preserving bonuses even as the Cosmic Age shifts. When a new Adept appears, consider how it complements your existing one. Overlapping Aspects with your Astral Houses create a strong Alignment advantage that rivals can't disrupt.

7. **Protect Your Progress with Wards:** Setting even 1–2 Reagents as Ward Reagents when you Fire makes opponents pay to challenge you. An unprotected Stone is a free target. Remember: Ward Reagents placed on a Crucible Card can also count toward satisfying that card's requirements.

8. **The Offering Rewards Everyone:** The Offering to the Age is a shared opportunity. Even rivals benefit from a successful Offering. If the Threshold looks reachable, contribute cards and nudge others to do the same — the 2-for-1 Crafting bonus is worth the cooperation.

## KISMETA CARDS

The Kismeta Deck (blue cover) is your primary tool across the Great Year. Its 134 cards divide into two types.

**Minor Arcana** (112 cards) - the building blocks of the Great Work. Each card has four traits:

- **Rank** - 2–10, plus Page (11), Knight (12), Queen (13), King (14), Ace (1 or 15)
- **Suit** - Wands (Fire/Red), Cups (Water/Blue), Pentacles (Earth/Green), Swords (Air/Yellow)
- **Planet** - one of seven planets; used for Aspect Alignments
- **Card Effects** - special effects that activate under conditions printed on the card

**Major Arcana (22 cards) - two varieties:**

| Specialist Cards (11)                                                   | Fate Cards (11)                                |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Persistent effect lasting across rounds                                 | One-time effect; discarded at round's end      |
| Carries three Aspects; used in Harvest & Alignments                     | No Aspects; effect only                        |
| Played into the Arcanum; hold up to 2                                   | Played into the Arcanum immediately when drawn |
| Must buy immediately when drawn, or discard - cannot hold and buy later | No purchase required; resolves automatically   |

Three concepts you'll use constantly:

- **Rank Points (RP)** - A card's numeric Rank. Add them together to find the combined Rank Sum of a card set, used when Crafting Reagents and in other contexts.
- **Card Sets** - Poker-style groupings of Kismeta Cards (Pairs, Three of a Kind, Flushes, Straights, etc.) assembled in your Spread to fulfill Cauldron Codex Formulas and other requirements.
- **Alchemical Alignments** - Precise card configurations specifying suits, planets, ranks, or combinations thereof that you must achieve in your Spread to satisfy an Alchemical Formula.

### 1.0 COSMIC AGES

There are 12 Cosmic Ages in the Great Year, each with a Zodiac Sign, Aspects, and a Cosmic Effect. Each round, this Age influences all Alchemists through its Aspects and Effect. The Aspects determine the Harvest phase conditions, and the Effect lasts for the round. Players can unlock up to four Cosmic Effects by building an Astral House on Zodiac Signs.

#### COSMIC EFFECT

When the Agekeeper sets the Cosmic Age at the start of Phase 1: Spring, all players immediately receive that Sign’s Cosmic Effect for the round. The effect applies only once and does not carry over to the next round. No Astral House is required.

#### ASTRAL HOUSE EFFECTS

Once unlocked, House Effects persist for the rest of the game. If you have houses on multiple Signs, you receive each unlocked effect. See Compendium: 1.7 Astral Houses

### 1.1 ASPECTS & ALIGNMENT

Aspects are Zodiac Signs, Planets, and Element/Card Suit pairings. Aspects appear on the Great Year board and on the cards. When two sources share a matching Aspect, it is called an Alignment. Aspect Alignment is the game's primary resource engine, generating larger Harvests, satisfying your Crucible Card requirements, and determining your performance in Oppositions, among other purposes.

#### ASPECTS

There are three Aspect types, each found across all three sources:

- **Zodiac Sign:** Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Leo, Virgo, Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius, Capricorn, Aquarius, Pisces
- **Planet:** Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn
- **Element/Card Suit:** Fire/Wands, Air/Swords, Water/Cups, Earth/Pentacles

Where to Find Aspects:

- Great Year Board: Cosmic Age Signs on the Zodiac Wheel
- Cards: Kismeta and Crucible Cards in your Spread, Hand, and Arcanum

Element, Card Suit & Color Connection:  
Each Element is tied to one Kismeta Card Suit, both sharing the same color.

- Elements are found on the Great Year board’s Zodiac Wheel
- Card Suits are found on the cards of the Kismeta Card deck

| ELEMENT | SUIT      | COLOR  |
| :------ | :-------- | :----- |
| Fire    | Wands     | Red    |
| Air     | Swords    | Yellow |
| Water   | Cups      | Blue   |
| Earth   | Pentacles | Green  |

#### ALIGNMENT

An Alignment occurs when two sources share an Aspect. When Alignments are scored, the result is called Alignment Points. One source is always the primary reference, typically the current Cosmic Age or Opposition Age, and all other sources are compared against it.

| MATCH TYPE | BONUS (Per Source) |
| :--------- | :----------------- |
| Sign       | +3                 |
| Planet     | +2                 |
| Element    | +1                 |
| None       | 0                  |

#### ALIGNMENT POINTS

When sources are compared to the primary source, each matching source earns Alignment Points based on its Highest-Matching Aspect. Alignment Points are used in two ways:

- **Alignment Bonus (Harvest):** Your total Alignment Points determine how many bonus cards you receive. See 1.2 Harvest.
- **Alignment Score (Oppositions):** Your total Alignment Points become your score. The player with the higher score wins. See 1.12 Opposition.

Highest-Matching Aspect:  
Each source is scored independently against the primary source. Only the single Highest-Matching Aspect counts per source: Sign (+3), Planet (+2), or Element (+1). You do not stack all three. All source totals are then added together to determine your final Alignment Points. This applies to both Harvest bonuses and Opposition scoring.

### 1.2 HARVEST

With every new Age comes a new Harvest, during which Alchemists collect the cards they’ll use to conduct their Great Work. All players automatically receive 2 cards at the start of each Harvest. Additionally, bonus cards are awarded based on how well each player aligns with the new Cosmic Age. Each player calculates their own bonus, and the Agekeeper confirms and deals the Harvest.

#### HARVEST SOURCES

- **Zodiac Die:** Each player rolls their personal Zodiac die. Compare the resulting Sign’s Aspects (Sign, Planet, Element) against the Cosmic Age’s Aspects. The Highest-Matching Aspect is awarded.
- **Astral Houses:** Each Astral House a player has built is checked against the Cosmic Age. Multiple Astral Houses may each earn a bonus independently, adding to the total amount.
- **Adept Cards:** Each active Adept card in your Arcanum carries three Aspects.
- **Spread Element Match:** Before the Harvest deal, each player counts the cards already in their Spread whose suit matches the Cosmic Age’s element (same color). Each earns +1 card.

#### ORDER OF OPERATIONS

Calculate Your Bonus Cards: Starting from the Base 2-card Harvest, each player adds any bonuses earned from the four Harvest Sources below. Add all bonuses.

- Base Harvest = 2 Cards every round
- Bonus Cards = Varies by player/round.
- Agekeeper’s Boon = +2 cards to all players, when the Agekeeper’s Zodiac Die result matches the current Cosmic Age Sign. See Phase 1: Spring

The Agekeeper deals: Once all players have tallied their totals, the Agekeeper deals each player their full Harvest amount face down. (Base + Bonus + Agekeeper’s Boon, if triggered.)
Commune with the Cards: Arrange all cards across each player’s Spread, Hand, and Arcanum.

⚠️ **If the Common Deck runs out during Harvest:** Immediately reshuffle the discard pile to form a new deck and continue dealing. If no discard pile is available, the Fates intervene: all players discard their entire Hand to the Common Deck, which is then reshuffled, and the Harvest continues from there.

#### EXAMPLE HARVEST:

The Cosmic Age is Aries (Aries, Mars, Fire).

- Base Harvest amount for all players: +2 cards
- You have 2 Wands(red) in your Spread, matching the Age’s Fire Element(red): +2 cards
- Your Zodiac sign is Leo (Fire), matching the Age’s Fire Element: +1 card
- You have an Astral House in Scorpio, matching the Age’s Mars Planet: +2 cards
- You have a Spread Card effect: “Earn +2 Bonus Cards if Aries is the Cosmic Age”: +2 cards
- Total Harvest Amount: 9 cards

### 1.3 KISMETA CARDS

The Kismeta Deck is your main tool during the Great Year, with 134 cards: 112 Minor Arcana, the building blocks of the Great Work, and 22 Major Arcana cards, with powerful one-time effects. Kismeta cards are essential for activating Crucible Cards, Refining Reagents, executing Alignments, and protecting your Stone in the Forge.

#### MAJOR ARCANA CARDS

Types of Major Arcana Cards:

- **Adept Cards:** There are 11 Adept Major Arcana Cards in the Common Deck, each with a unique effect that lasts for rounds. You can buy a card at its listed price or discard it. You may hold up to two Adept Cards and swap them by paying the listed price and returning the old card to the deck. Adept Cards are played into the Arcanum zone.
  - Aspects: Each Adept card has a set of three Aspects. These can be used during the Harvest to generate additional bonus cards.
  - ⚠️ Important: When an Adept card is drawn, you must buy it immediately or discard it. You cannot hold it in your Hand and purchase it later.
- **Fate Cards:** There are 11 Fate Major Arcana Cards. Each has a powerful one-time effect applied to your Arcanum zone, resolved by following the card’s directions, then discarded at the round’s end.

#### MINOR ARCANA CARDS

Traits of a Minor Arcana Card:

- **Rank:** Each card carries a numerical rank. 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10,
- **Court Cards:** Page (11), Knight (12), Queen (13), King (14), Ace (1 OR 15)
- **Suit:** Wands (Fire), Cups (Water), Pentacles (Earth), and Swords (Air). Each shares the same color as one Element and one Reagent type.
- **Planet:** Each card is assigned a Planet.
- **Card Effects:** Kismeta Cards carry special effects that activate under specific conditions. See Kismeta Card Effects below.

#### CARD SETS, RANK POINTS & ALCHEMICAL ALIGNMENTS

- **Card Sets:** Poker-style groupings of Kismeta Cards (Pairs, Three Of A Kind, Flushes, Straights, etc.) collected in the Spread to fulfill Crucible Codex Formulae, Alignments, etc.
- **Rank Points:** Each card’s numeric Rank. Add these ranks together to find the Total Rank Sum of a card set, used to refine Reagents, and in other areas of the game.
- **Alchemical Alignments** are precise card configurations that an Alchemist must achieve in their Spread to satisfy part of an Alchemical Formula, found on the Crucible Cards.

#### HAND, SPREAD & ARCANUM: Managing Your Cards

You manage your cards across three main zones: the Spread, the Hand, and the Arcanum.

- **Spread: (Visible: Active Effects)** There is no card limit on the Spread during the course of a round. The 7-card maximum is enforced only at the end of the round during Phase 4: Winter. Cards added to the Spread mid-round (from Duels, card effects, or other sources) may exceed this limit temporarily. The Spread is your active area, where cards with effects remain active while in it: activating Crucible Cards, meeting Forge Alignment, refining Reagents, building Astral Houses, trading, wagering, opposing, and targeting in Duels.
- **Hand: (Hidden; Inactive Effects)** Up to 5 private cards are hidden from opponents. Hand cards may be used alongside Spread cards for Reagent Refinement; used Hand cards are discarded. Hand cards may also contribute to an Opposition, but are discarded after the Opposition resolves, regardless of outcome. These cards can’t be used for Duels, Crucible, Alignments, Trades, Wagers, or Houses.
- **Arcanum: (Visible; Active Major Arcana Cards)** Any active Major Arcana cards. Fate cards are always placed face-up in the Arcanum immediately when drawn, even if dealt as part of a private face-down Harvest. Adept cards must be purchased and can be added in up to two at a time.

#### KISMETA CARD EFFECTS

Kismeta Cards have special effects that activate under specific conditions and stay in your Spread. They are not discarded after use and can be reused as specified.

Aligning Cards to Activate Effects:

- **Passive Effects:** Some Kismeta Cards have effects that need no additional interaction to be activated. They remain active as long as they remain in the Spread.
- **Active Effects:** Some Kismeta Card effects require specific Aspect pairings among two or more cards in your Spread to activate. Follow the instructions on the card to activate.

**Individual Card Dynamics**

##### The Tower — The Collapse — Hand Redistribution

**Core Dynamic:** All Hand cards from all players are collected by the Agekeeper, shuffled together, and dealt one at a time clockwise until all cards are distributed. Players may receive more cards than their Hand limit during redistribution; limits are enforced at Winter as normal.

**Key Tension:** A total randomization of private reserves — no player can protect against it. Players who have hoarded strong Hand cards may lose them; players with weak Hands may gain a windfall. Because all Hands are pooled, larger tables create wilder swings.

**Mechanical Interactions:** Cards received from The Tower are subject to Card Lock and cannot move to the Spread until Winter. The Hermit Adept's bonus Hand card is included in the pool. Applies to all players regardless of Hand size — a player with 0 Hand cards participates but simply may or may not receive cards.

📋 **Design Note:** Confirm: does a player with 0 Hand cards still participate in the clockwise deal (receiving cards)? Recommend yes.

---

##### Death — The Great Transformer — Immediate Action ⚠️ Needs Confirmation

**Core Dynamic:** The player who drew this card discards their entire Spread to the discard pile. The Agekeeper then shuffles the Common Deck.

**Key Tension:** Targets only the drawer — catastrophic for a player mid-Summer who has built a strong Spread. All Alignment sources, Gambit eligibility, and Crucible Card support are lost instantly. The deck shuffle resets card state for everyone.

**Mechanical Interactions:** All Spread cards are lost, including those contributing to active Crucible Card Alignment requirements. Hand cards are unaffected. Any declared Gambits that relied on the drawer's Spread are nullified at the moment of discard.

📋 **Open Ruling:** Does "shuffle the deck" include the just-discarded Spread cards, or only the existing discard pile? Recommend including them — it resets the economy for everyone.

---

##### The Sun — All Is Revealed — Immediate Action

**Core Dynamic:** All players immediately reveal their full Hand to all other players. Hands remain visible to all players until Phase 4: Winter's Card Unlock step.

**Key Tension:** Eliminates all information asymmetry from hidden Hand cards for the rest of the round. Affects every negotiation that follows: Trade leverage, Opposition planning, Duel targeting, and Fateful Wager bluffing all lose their hidden dimension.

**Mechanical Interactions:** Cards added to the Hand after The Sun resolves (e.g., from The Star's second Harvest) are revealed immediately upon being placed there. Players with empty Hands are unaffected. Weakens any Adept effect that relies on Hand secrecy.

📋 **Design Note:** New design — not from playtest. Confirm: does this affect Fateful Wager cards placed face-down in front of players? Recommend no — Wager cards are not Hand cards.

---

##### Judgement — The Final Reckoning — Immediate Action ⚠️ Needs Confirmation

**Core Dynamic:** The Agekeeper names any one Reagent type (Sulphur, Aqua Regia, Vitriol, or Quicksilver). All players who currently hold none of that Reagent type must immediately discard 2 Spread cards of their choice.

**Key Tension:** The Agekeeper has genuine targeting power — they can name the Reagent that hits the most players, or aim strategically at a rival. Players with diversified Reagent stores are safe; specialists or early-game players with empty stores are exposed.

**Mechanical Interactions:** Do Protective Ward Reagents (placed on Crucible Cards or Forge positions) exempt a player? Recommend yes — Wards count as held Reagents.

📋 **Open Ruling:** Should the Agekeeper be required to name the Reagent before surveying the table, to prevent optimal targeting? A blind choice adds fairness; an informed choice adds strategy.

---

##### Justice — The Scales Tipped — Threshold

**Core Dynamic:** The player holding the most total Reagents (any type combined) must give 1 Reagent of the recipient's choice to the player holding the fewest. Ties for most: each tied player gives 1. Ties for fewest: the giving player chooses who receives.

**Key Tension:** Penalizes Reagent hoarding at the moment of drawing. The recipient's-choice-of-type rule means the giver loses their most useful Reagent, not their surplus. Players who spread Reagents into Protective Wards may fall below the threshold.

**Mechanical Interactions:** Do Ward Reagents count toward a player's total? Recommend yes — consistency with Judgement ruling. Single transfer only, not an ongoing redistribution.

---

##### The Moon — The Gift of Tides — Social Action

**Core Dynamic:** The player who drew this card gives any 1 card from their Spread to the player on their left. They then receive any 1 card from the Spread of the player on their right.

**Key Tension:** You give before you receive and cannot negotiate — the direction is fixed. Strategic players may give a card that benefits their left neighbor least, or give generously to signal alliance before Opposition season.

**Mechanical Interactions:** Cards received via The Moon enter the Spread (face-up, subject to Card Lock). The drawer cannot refuse either transfer. Cards moved this way cannot move to the Hand until Winter's Card Unlock. In a 2-player game, the same player is both left and right — the drawer gives and receives from the single opponent (effectively a swap).

---

##### The Fool — Into the Unknown — Collective Action

**Core Dynamic:** The Agekeeper draws 1 card from the top of the Crucible Deck and places it face-up in the center of the table as a Collective Crucible Card. Any player may work toward completing it using their own resources during the Summer phase. The first player to satisfy its requirements claims it and resolves its effect.

**Key Tension:** Introduces a moment of shared competition for a single card. Players must decide whether to divert resources to compete for the Collective Card or stay focused on their personal Crucible progress.

**Mechanical Interactions:** The Collective Card uses standard Crucible activation rules — the claiming player discards the required card set. If unclaimed by end of Phase 4: Winter, it is discarded.

📋 **Open Ruling:** Does claiming the Collective Card award a Molten Coal drop as normal? Recommend yes. Does the card draw from standard Codex formula positions or is it standalone? Recommend standalone — it activates based on its own printed formula.

---

##### The Devil — The Temptation — Forced Exchange

**Core Dynamic:** The player who drew this card names any 1 card currently in any opponent's Spread. That opponent must give the named card to the drawer immediately. The opponent then draws 2 replacement cards from the Common Deck, placed directly into their Spread.

**Key Tension:** A coercive targeted steal with built-in compensation. The drawer gets exactly the card they want; the defender loses a specific asset but gains two unknowns.

**Mechanical Interactions:** Only Spread cards may be targeted — Hand cards are safe. The named card must be visibly in the Spread at the moment of naming. The 2 replacement cards drawn by the defender are subject to Card Lock. This is not a Trade — The Magician Adept's trade rules do not apply.

---

##### Wheel of Fortune — The Ages Turn — Dice Event ⚠️ Needs Confirmation

**Core Dynamic:** All players immediately re-roll their Zodiac Dice and move Meeples to the new Signs. The player who rolls highest gains 2 Reagents of their choice from the supply. The player who rolls lowest must discard all but 1 card from their Spread. Ties for highest or lowest: reroll among tied players only.

**Key Tension:** A full Alignment reset combined with genuine high-stakes dice drama. Players who built their Spread to align with their current Sign are suddenly misaligned.

**Mechanical Interactions:** All Alignment calculations for the remainder of the round (Oppositions, Gambits, Dueling bonuses) use the new Sign. Harvest bonuses already distributed are not recalculated. Astral Houses still contribute their fixed Alignment regardless of the re-roll result.

📋 **Open Ruling:** Confirm severity for the lowest roller — "all but 1 Spread card" is severe. Softer alternative: discard down to 3 Spread cards. Worth testing both versions.

---

##### The Hanged Man — Suspended Between Worlds — Hand Redistribution

**Core Dynamic:** All players simultaneously pass their entire Hand to the player on their left.

**Key Tension:** Sudden loss of private reserves and strategic plans. Players who have hoarded specific cards for an Opposition, Refinement, or Fateful Wager must now work with whatever their right neighbor was holding.

**Mechanical Interactions:** Cards received become the new owner's Hand immediately and are subject to Card Lock. The Hermit Adept's bonus Hand card is included in the pass. In a 2-player game, both players simply swap Hands.

---

##### The Star — A Guiding Light — Boon

**Core Dynamic:** The player who drew this card conducts a full second Harvest immediately. Recalculate all Alignment bonuses using current Zodiac Signs and Aspects; deal base cards and all Bonus Cards as normal. These cards are placed in Spread or Hand during a second Commune step.

**Key Tension:** A pure positive Fate — the drawer gains a significant card advantage. No decision required beyond zone placement during the second Commune.

**Mechanical Interactions:** Uses current Zodiac Signs at the moment The Star is drawn — if Wheel of Fortune has already re-rolled Signs this round, the second Harvest reflects the new Signs. Agekeeper's Boon applies if conditions are met. Cards drawn in the second Harvest are subject to Card Lock as normal.

**Individual Card Dynamics**

##### The Magician — Master of All Elements — Trade / Reagents

**Core Dynamic:** While this card is in your Arcanum, Reagents of any type may be used interchangeably to satisfy Crucible Card activation requirements. You may substitute any Reagent type for any other when paying an activation cost.

**Key Tension:** Removes the color-matching constraint on Crucible activation. Lets you use your most abundant Reagent type regardless of what the Crucible Card demands — smoothing out resource imbalances.

**Mechanical Interactions:** Applies only to Crucible Card activation costs, not to Forge Firing costs (Firing Reagents must still match as printed on the card). Does not affect Refinement costs, Trade mechanics, or Protective Ward costs.

📋 **Open Ruling:** Confirm scope: activation only, not Firing.

##### The High Priestess — Keeper of Secrets — Hand Management 📋 OPEN — NEEDS DESIGN

**Thematic Focus:** Hidden knowledge, secrets, expanded capacity. Should reward players who build and protect a deep Hand reserve.

**Candidate Directions:**

- **(A)** Expanded Hand limit — hold up to 8 cards instead of 5.
- **(B)** Recover Hand cards contributed to a winning Opposition rather than discarding them.
- **(C)** Once per round, look at one opponent's Hand without revealing your own.

📋 **Design Note:** High priority — Hand management is underserved in the current Adept set. Option A is simplest and most practical. Option B rewards aggressive Opposition play. Option C creates an information asymmetry but may slow the table.

##### The Empress — The Fertile Cauldron — Reagent / Abundance 📋 OPEN — NEEDS DESIGN

**Thematic Focus:** Fertility, abundance, growth. Should reward players who invest deeply in Reagent production — distinct from The Magician (flexibility) and Temperance (suit blending).

**Candidate Directions:**

- **(A)** Once per round, when you successfully refine a Reagent, immediately refine a second from the same Cauldron at −5 RP.
- **(B)** Gain 1 bonus Reagent of your choice each time you contribute to a successful Offering to the Age.
- **(C)** Reduce the RP threshold of one chosen Cauldron by a permanent 5 RP (stacks with Molten Coal).

📋 **Design Note:** Should emphasize output and abundance rather than flexibility. Option C creates a permanent board-state change that could be very powerful. Avoid repeating The Magician's interchangeability pattern.

##### The Emperor — Sovereign of the Forge — Opposition / Defense ⚠️ Needs Confirmation

**Core Dynamic:** Protective Wards placed beside your Forging Stone are not discarded when you lose an Opposition. They remain in place for the next round. Wards placed on Crucible Cards still follow standard rules.

**Key Tension:** Dramatically lowers the cost of defending in the Forge — you can invest heavily in Forge Wards without fear of losing them on a bad roll. Makes committing to an early Forge entry far less risky.

**Mechanical Interactions:** When a Stone is sent to Stasis, persisted Wards remain beside the Forge position and are still active when the Stone returns. Does not protect Crucible Card Wards — only Forge Stone Wards benefit.

📋 **Open Ruling:** When a Stone returns from Stasis to the Forge, are persisted Wards still active at the shield symbol? Recommend yes.

##### The Hierophant — High Keeper of the Arcanum — Arcanum / Capacity 📋 OPEN — NEEDS DESIGN

**Thematic Focus:** Tradition, initiation, holding more than others can. Should expand what the player can maintain in their Arcanum.

**Candidate Directions:**

- **(A)** Hold up to 3 Adept cards in the Arcanum simultaneously (instead of 2).
- **(B)** Once per round, swap one Adept card for another at no cost — pay the incoming card's cost as normal but recover the outgoing card's cost as a Reagent credit.
- **(C)** Adept cards in your Arcanum cannot be affected by Fate card effects.

📋 **Design Note:** The 3-Adept capacity is most intuitive thematically. Main balance concern: 3 Adepts contribute 3 full Aspect sets to Harvest scoring — watch for Harvest inflation. Consider: 3rd Adept contributes its persistent effect only, not its Aspects for Harvest scoring.

##### The Lovers — The Harmonious Union — Trade / Social

**Core Dynamic:** When this card is drawn and purchased, choose 1 other player. That player decides for you both: either both players draw 2 Kismeta Cards from the Common Deck, or both players gain 1 Reagent of their individual choice.

**Key Tension:** You choose the partner; they choose the reward. Players may pick a partner likely to choose the option that benefits themselves — or gamble on a generous partner.

**Mechanical Interactions:** Cards drawn via The Lovers enter Spread or Hand normally and are subject to Card Lock. Reagents gained are taken from the general supply. The partner's choice applies equally to both. Note: this effect triggers at the moment of purchase — it is not a recurring round effect.

##### The Chariot — The Unstoppable Will — Duel ⚠️ Needs Confirmation

**Core Dynamic:** When you win a Duel, you may take 2 cards from the Defender's Spread instead of 1 (declare both target cards before rolling). If you lose a Duel, your Ante Card is returned to your Spread rather than being discarded.

**Key Tension:** Transforms Dueling into a high-upside, low-downside proposition. Pre-declaring 2 targets raises the psychological stakes for both players. The Ante protection enables repeated aggressive Dueling at minimal personal cost.

**Mechanical Interactions:** Both target cards must be in the Defender's Spread at the moment of declaration. If one card is removed before resolution, the Challenger claims only the remaining named target. Ante Card returns to the Spread on loss (not Hand). The Devil Fate bypasses Duel rolls — Chariot's Ante protection does not apply to Devil steals.

📋 **Open Ruling:** Keep Duel focus (take 2 cards on win, Ante returned on loss) or revert to original "Wildcard in Oppositions"?

##### Strength — Inner Fire — Opposition / Resilience 📋 OPEN — NEEDS DESIGN

**Candidate Directions:**

- **(A)** Once per round, after any dice roll in a Duel or Opposition (not Gambit), discard 2 Hand cards to add +2 to your roll or Alignment score — declared before results are compared.
- **(B)** Pay 2 Sulphur Reagents to cancel any one card effect applied to you (single use per purchase).
- **(C)** Reroll your Zodiac Die once per round in any Duel or Opposition before results are compared.

📋 **Design Note:** Original Crucible Card condition was Option B — thematic, practical, and creates a meaningful Reagent sink. Leading candidate.

##### The Hermit — The Solitary Path — Harvest / Hand

**Core Dynamic:** At the start of each Harvest (before Bonus Cards are dealt), draw 1 additional card from the Common Deck directly into your Hand. This card may be moved freely between Hand and Spread during Phase 1: Commune only, before Card Lock applies.

**Key Tension:** A private resource accumulated over multiple rounds that rivals cannot track. The Commune-window flexibility lets you evaluate the card against your current Spread state before committing — without creating a permanently special-rule card.

**Mechanical Interactions:** Disrupted by The Sun Fate (Hand revealed) and The Hanged Man Fate (pass Hands left). The Tower Fate includes this card in the Hand shuffle. The free movement window closes the moment Card Lock is declared in Spring Step 5.

##### Temperance — The Alchemical Blend — Reagent / Cauldron

**Core Dynamic:** You may satisfy a Reagent Refinement using cards from two different suits. The combined RP must meet the Threshold of the Cauldron matching the Reagent type you choose to produce. You receive 1 Reagent of your chosen type. May only be used once per turn.

**Key Tension:** Breaks the strict suit-to-Reagent correspondence. Lets you convert surplus from one element into a different Reagent — smoothing the economy when your Harvest is misaligned with your Crucible needs.

**Mechanical Interactions:** The Reagent type produced must correspond to one of the two suits used — you cannot blend Wands and Cups to produce Vitriol. The Threshold used is that of the Cauldron matching the produced Reagent, with all Molten Coal reductions applied.

📋 **Open Ruling:** Can a player Temperance-blend AND also standard-refine from one of the same suits in the same turn? Recommend yes — these are separate Refinement actions.

##### The World — The Great Work Perfected — Crucible / Forge ⚠️ Needs Confirmation

**Core Dynamic:** When you successfully Temper your Stone (advance from Forge to Mantle Ring), you may immediately flip your next Crucible Card face-up for free — it becomes Active without discarding the required card set. You still must satisfy its full Alchemical Formula before Firing.

**Key Tension:** Removes the activation cost for the next Crucible Card — not the Firing cost. The card set that would have been discarded remains in your Spread, available for Alignments, Refinement, or Gambits. Creates a substantial tempo advantage at mid-game stages.

**Mechanical Interactions:** Most powerful at Stages 2 and 3 where Crucible Card activation costs are heavier. Has no effect at Stage 4 — Tempering at Stage 4 wins the game. The next card in position order activates (I, II, III, IV); cannot skip a position.

📋 **Open Ruling:** Keep "free Crucible activation on Temper" or revert to original "Start Oppositions One Sign Ahead when Challenging"?

#### MINOR ARCANA CARD REFERENCE

The 112 Minor Arcana cards form the core resource of the Kismeta deck. Because Kismeta uses two full tarot decks, every Suit/Rank combination exists as **two distinct physical cards** (Card 1 and Card 2) with different effects.

| **Card 1 — Active Effect + Spread Alignment**                                                         | **Card 2 — Wildcard Link + Active Effect**                                                                      |
| :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Carries a base Active Effect (always on while in Spread or Hand as applicable)                        | Carries a Wildcard Link to a specific Major Arcana card; may substitute for that card in all requirements       |
| May also carry a Spread Alignment — a conditional secondary effect triggered by cards in your Spread  | Carries a standalone Active Effect of its own, independent of the Wildcard function                             |
| Curse cards (Ranks 4, 5, 6) have negative Active Effects; their Alignment conditions negate the curse | Court cards (Page, Knight, Queen, King) C2 effects are unique per card and generally stronger than numbered C2s |
| No Wildcard Link                                                                                      | Queens C2 and Kings C2 have no Wildcard Link — they carry unique standalone effects only                        |

**Spread Alignment**

Each Card 1 Minor Arcana may carry a **Spread Alignment** — a conditional trigger that activates a secondary effect when a specific type of card is present elsewhere in your Spread. Spread Alignment has two components: the **Alignment Condition** (what must be in your Spread) and the **Alignment Effect** (what activates when the condition is met).

| **Condition Type** | **Description**                                                                                    |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Planet Align**   | Any card in your Spread whose Planet symbol matches the stated planet, regardless of Suit or Rank. |
| **Suit Align**     | Any card in your Spread of the stated Suit.                                                        |
| **Rank Align**     | Any card in your Spread of the stated Rank, regardless of Suit or Planet.                          |

| **Effect Type** | **Description**                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| :-------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Negation**    | Cancels the card's Active Effect entirely. Always applies to curse cards (Ranks 4, 5, 6). No curse is unplayable — every curse has a negation condition requiring deliberate Spread construction. |
| **Enhancement** | Adds a bonus to or upgrades the card's existing effect. Some Enhancement Alignments activate an effect the card doesn't have on its own.                                                          |

**Core Rules**

| **Rule**                    | **Detail**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| :-------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Self-exclusion**          | A card cannot satisfy its own Spread Alignment condition. The triggering card must be a separate card already in the Spread.                                                                                                                               |
| **Spread-only**             | Only cards in your Spread count. Cards in your Hand, Arcanum, or committed to a Fateful Wager do not satisfy Spread Alignment conditions.                                                                                                                  |
| **Continuous**              | Spread Alignment is checked continuously. It activates the moment a qualifying card enters your Spread and deactivates the moment that card leaves. If a Mercury card that was negating a curse is discarded mid-Summer, the curse resumes immediately.    |
| **Stacking**                | If multiple cards share the same Alignment condition, a single qualifying card satisfies all of them simultaneously. One Mercury card in your Spread negates every Mercury Align curse you are carrying.                                                   |
| **Passive / Active timing** | Passive effects (curse negations, Threshold reductions, Trade bonuses) require no announcement. Active effects that modify dice rolls (Pages, Knights, 7s) are declared at the moment of the relevant action and confirmed by showing the qualifying card. |

**Design Notes — Intentional Patterns**

| **Pattern**                             | **Detail**                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| :-------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **4s — Universal Negation**             | All four 4s share Mercury Align as their negation condition regardless of Suit. Mercury is the universal negotiator in alchemical tradition — a single Mercury card in your Spread protects against any 4 curse you carry. |
| **5s — Cross-Element Pairs**            | The 5s use cross-element suit pairings as negation conditions: Cups 5 by Swords, Pentacles 5 by Wands, Swords 5 by Cups, Wands 5 by Pentacles. These are opposite-element pairs (Water/Air, Earth/Fire).                   |
| **Swords & Wands 4s — Different Curse** | Cups and Pentacles 4s curse opponents with +2 cards on Duel wins. Swords and Wands 4s instead force the holder to offer two Ante Cards to begin a Duel. Both are Mercury Align negated.                                    |
| **3s — Reagent Payment**                | The 3 Spread Alignment replaces the payment method for Astral Houses: instead of 2 Planet-matching cards, the holder pays 1 Reagent of the matching type.                                                                  |
| **Queens — Moon Align**                 | All Queens share Moon Align as their Alignment condition, enhancing Reagent doubling when another Moon influence is present in the Spread.                                                                                 |
| **Kings — Venus Align**                 | All Kings share Venus Align as their enhancement condition, triggering their double-output Refinement effect twice per round when a Venus card is in the Spread.                                                           |

### 1.4 CRUCIBLE CARDS

The Fates inscribed twenty-two Crucible Cards for the Great Work, each linked to a Major Arcana card and shaping its Formula. Four cards are used in the game, one for each Stage of Transmutation. Initially dormant, each card has a Molten Coal on top, hiding its Alchemical Formula until activation.

#### CRUCIBLE CARD STATES

Each Crucible Card has four states that track the card’s position and your actions. They relate to advancing the Philosopher’s Stone, which has separate states. See Compendium: 1.11 Crucible & Philosopher’s Stone

- **Dormant:** (Card face-down, Molten Coal resting atop it.) The Formula lies hidden.
- **Active:** (Card face-up, Molten Coal removed.) You are working toward the Formula. Multiple Crucible Cards may be Active simultaneously.
  - 💡 Tip: You can activate multiple Crucible Cards at once. Getting ahead on future Formulae gives you time to accumulate the cards and Reagents you will need before each Stage.
- **Fired:** (Card face-up, turned sideways.) The Alchemical Formula is executed, and the Transmutation is underway, forging the Philosopher’s Coal in the Crucible. You may only have one Crucible Card Fired at a time.
- **Discarded:** The Crucible Card is discarded when the Coal is Tempered to the next Mantle Ring. The Stage is complete.
- ⚠️ Important: You may only have one Crucible Card Fired at a time. If your Stone is already Forging, you must Temper it before you can fire again.

#### ACTIVATING A CRUCIBLE CARD

To activate a Crucible Card, follow these steps. You may do this during Phase 2: Summer or Phase 3: Autumn:

- **Consult the Crucible Codex:** Your Codex holds four Formulae handed down by the Fates. One formula for each card position (I, II, III, and IV, left to right). Find the formula that matches the position of the card you wish to activate.
- **Choose a Crucible Card to Activate:** You may Activate them in any order, and any number may be Active simultaneously.
- **Collect the Cards:** Assemble the required card set in your Spread and discard it.
- **Activate the Crucible Card:** Extract the Molten Coal from the card and drop it into any Crucible Cauldron of your choice. The flame burns hotter, and that Cauldron’s Threshold permanently drops by 5 RP. Flip the Crucible Card face-up. It is now Active, and its Formula has been revealed. See Compendium: 1.6 Reagents

#### THE ALCHEMICAL FORMULA

Every Crucible Card presents an Alchemical Formula, the sacred set of demands the Fates have placed upon this Stage of the Transmutation process. Each Formula has three components:

- **Reagents:** The specific alchemical substances (Sulphur, Aqua Regia, Vitriol, and/or Quicksilver) that must be discarded to Fuel the Forge and begin the Transmutation. The Reagent cost is listed on the Crucible Card.
- **Alignment:** The precise card configuration you must hold in your Spread to execute the Formula. This may specify suits, planets, ranks, or any combination thereof. The Alignment must be confirmed before the Stone can be Fired.

### 1.5 CRUCIBLE CAULDRONS & MOLTEN COAL

Raising the heat of the Crucible fires. Each Molten Coal is extracted from a Crucible Card by collecting one of the Card Sets listed on each player’s Crucible Codex. See Compendium: 1.4 Crucible Cards

#### CRUCIBLE CAULDRONS

You have four Crucible Cauldrons, located at the corners of their section of the board. These Cauldrons are color-coded to match the four Elements and their corresponding suits. The symbol for the Card Suit can be found on each of the Cauldrons on the game board. Cauldrons are used to store Molten Coal, extracted from Dormant Crucible Cards.

#### MOLTEN COAL: Fueling the Cauldrons

Molten Coal is extracted from Dormant Crucible Cards. Dropping a Molten Coal into a Cauldron stokes its flame, making the Crucible’s fire burn hotter and the Refinement process more efficient. Each Molten Coal permanently lowers that specific Cauldron’s Rank Point (RP) Threshold by 5 RP.

- ⚠️ Important: Molten Coals cannot be moved once placed.
- Four Cauldrons: Blue, Green, Red, Yellow

### 1.6 REAGENTS

Reagents are the concentrated alchemical fuels required to power the Great Work. While Kismeta Cards represent raw potential gathered from the cosmos, Reagents are the refined energy needed to "Fuel the Forge" and advance your Philosopher’s Stone through the Crucible.

#### FOUR REAGENTS

There are four types of Reagents, each refined from a specific suit of Kismeta Cards:

| REAGENT     | REQ’D SUIT TO REFINE | CAULDRON |
| :---------- | :------------------- | :------- |
| Sulphur     | Wands                | Red      |
| Quicksilver | Swords               | Yellow   |
| Aqua Regia  | Cups                 | Blue     |
| Vitriol     | Pentacles            | Green    |

#### REFINING REAGENTS

Refinement is the process of trading in cards to receive a Reagent token. You may do this any number of times during Phase 2: Summer.

| # OF COALS IN A CAULDRON | RP THRESHOLD (Per Suit) |
| :----------------------- | :---------------------- |
| 0                        | ≥ 30 RP                 |
| 1                        | ≥ 25 RP                 |
| 2                        | ≥ 20 RP                 |
| 3                        | ≥ 15 RP                 |
| 4                        | ≥ 10 RP                 |

- **The Threshold:** To create one Reagent, you must discard a set of cards of the matching suit whose combined Rank Points (RP) meet or exceed the current Total Rank Sum Threshold. The default Threshold for all Reagents is 30 Rank Points. This can be lowered by 5 RP for every Molten Coal placed in the corresponding Crucible Cauldron on the game board.
- **Using Your Full Inventory:** You may use cards from your Spread and your Hand to reach the required point total.
- **The Exchange:** Once the Threshold is met, discard the cards to the common pile and take one matching Reagent token from the supply.

💡 **Tip:** Each Molten Coal placed in a Cauldron permanently lowers that Cauldron’s Threshold by 5 RP each. They cannot be moved once placed. Choose wisely.

#### EXAMPLE: Refining A Reagent

You want to refine 1 Sulphur (Red) Reagent.
Your Red Cauldron has 2 Molten Coals, lowering the Threshold to 20 RP.
Your Cards:

- 6 of Wands = 6 RP
- 8 of Wands = 8 RP
- Knight of Wands = 11 RP
- Total RP: 25 RP: satisfies the 20 RP Threshold.
  Result: Discard all three cards and take 1 Sulphur token from the supply.

If you fall short: If you only held the 6 and 8 of Wands (14 RP), you fall short and cannot refine. Acquire more Wands cards before attempting again.

⚙️ **Game Mode Modifier: (Quickplay)** In Quickplay Mode, all Cauldrons share one simplified Threshold. Each Molten Coal placed in any Cauldron reduces the cost for all Reagents regardless of color. See GAME MODES: Quickplay.

### 1.7 ASTRAL HOUSES

Astral Houses are permanent structures built directly on the Zodiac Wheel of the Great Year board. They serve as additional sources in your Harvest, strengthen your Oppositions, and unlock a permanent Cosmic Effect that stacks for the rest of the game. Once built, they remain permanent. Only one Astral House can be built per sign.

- ⚠️ Important: Only one player may build an Astral House on any given Sign. However, Astral Houses do not prevent other players from placing their Meeple on that Sign if they roll it as their Zodiac Sign. Players may still use their Zodiac Sign to collect Bonus Harvest cards or in any Alignment.

#### BUILDING AN ASTRAL HOUSE

- **Cost:** Pay 2 Kismeta Cards sharing the Sign’s Planet.
- **Placement:** Place your 🏰 Astral House token on the 🏰 symbol on the Sign where your Meeple currently stands. Once placed, the House is permanent and cannot be moved.

#### WHAT AN ASTRAL HOUSE DOES

An Astral House on a Sign provides three permanent benefits, each operating independently.

- **Bonus Cards:** Your Astral House grants bonus cards during Spring's Harvest in Phase 1 if its Sign, Planet, or Element matches the current Cosmic Age. You get +1 for Element, +2 for Planet, +3 for Sign, stacking with Zodiac die results, Spread Element Match, and active effects.
- **Opposition Alignment:** Your Astral House serves as an Alignment source in Oppositions, contributing to your Alignment Points when matching the Opposition Age: +3 for Sign, +2 for Planet, and +1 for Element. This applies whether you are the Challenger or the Defender.
- **Cosmic Effect:** When you build an Astral House on a Sign, you permanently unlock that Sign’s Cosmic Effect for yourself. This effect applies to you at the start of every Phase 1: Spring, regardless of the current Cosmic Age. See Compendium: 1.0 Cosmic Ages.

### 1.8 TRADES

Alchemists who deal wisely with their rivals can convert surplus cards into exactly what they need. During Phase 2: Summer, you may trade any of the following resources:

- Kismeta Cards: These can also be dueled over.
- Reagents: These cannot be exchanged any other way.
- Active Crucible Cards: These cannot be exchanged any other way.

You may not trade the following at any time:

- Astral Houses
- Protective Wards
- Dormant Crucible Cards
- Molten Coals

📌 **Note:** Trading is the only way to exchange Reagents and Active Crucible Cards between players. They cannot be won in Duels or transferred any other way.

⚙️ **Game Mode Modifier: (Magnus Alchemist)** Misaligned players must trade 2:1 in favor of the non-initiating player. The initiating player is the one who proposes the trade. See GAME MODES: Magnus Alchemist.

### 1.9 DUELS

A Duel lets you challenge a rival to steal a card from their Spread during Phase 2: Summer.

- **Declare the Duel:** Declare your opponent and the card you are targeting.
- **Entry Fee:** Choose either Ante Card or Harvest Loan
  - **Ante Card:** Offer one card from your Spread as your Ante. If you lose, this card is returned to the Common Deck.
  - **Harvest Loan:** Take a Harvest Loan against your next round’s Harvest. You may take up to 2 Harvest Loans per round. If you lose, the loan is deducted from your Base Harvest of 2 cards next round — not from your bonus cards. A single loan reduces your Base Harvest to 1 card; two loans reduce it to 0. Bonus cards from Alignment are unaffected. The Agekeeper tracks outstanding loans. A player who has already taken 2 Harvest Loans and wishes to initiate a further Duel that round must use an Ante Card as their entry fee. Only the initiating player pays the entry fee; the Defender does not.
- **Consult Your Spread:** Both players look for any Spread Effects that may be relevant.
- **The Clash:** Both players roll their Player Dice.
- **Determine the Outcome:**
  - **Challenger Wins:** Take any one card from the rival’s Spread.
  - **Defender Wins:**
    - Ante Card: The Ante Card is returned to the Common Deck.
    - Harvest Loan: One card is deducted from the Challenger’s Base Harvest next round.
  - **Tie:** Both players reroll their dice. Repeat until one player rolls higher than the other. There are no ties in a Duel.

⚙️ **Game Mode Modifier: (Magnus Alchemist)** If you are misaligned with your target when initiating a Duel, add +1 to your dice roll before comparing results. The Defender receives no bonus. See GAME MODES: Magnus Alchemist.

#### EXAMPLE: A Duel

You challenge a rival and target the King of Cups in their Spread.
You offer a 5 of Wands as your Ante Card.
The Roll:

- You roll: 9
- Your rival rolls: 4
- Challenger wins (9 > 4): Take the King of Cups from the rival’s Spread. Your Ante Card is returned to you.
- Defender wins (if you roll lower): Your 5 of Wands Ante Card is returned to the Common Deck. You may not challenge the same Defender again this round.

### 1.10 GAMBIT

A Gambit is a challenge declared during Phase 2: Summer, in which a Challenger proves mastery of a rival’s Active Crucible Card and seizes it, forcing a swap from their own inventory.

#### WHEN A GAMBIT CAN BE DECLARED

A Gambit may only be declared during Phase 2: Summer, on the Challenger’s turn, targeting an Active Crucible Card. The Challenger must have the target card’s full Alignment requirement satisfied in their Spread at the moment of declaration.

#### DECLARING A GAMBIT: Order Of Play

- **Declare the Gambit:** The Challenger identifies the target card and demonstrates that their Spread satisfies its full Alignment requirement. The Challenger names which of their own Active Crucible Cards they are offering in exchange. The Challenger pays the bank Reagents equal to the Protective Ward on the targeted card. No Ward = no entry fee.
- **The Clash:** Both players roll their Zodiac Dice. Apply any card effects that modify Duels or Gambits before comparing results.
- ⚙️ **Game Mode Modifier: (Magnus Alchemist)** If Misaligned with the Defender at declaration, add +1 to the Challenger’s dice roll. The Defender receives no bonus. See GAME MODES: Magnus Alchemist.

#### OUTCOMES

- **Challenger Wins:** The two Active Crucible Cards are swapped. Each card moves into the position vacated by the other. The Defender’s Protective Ward Reagents are spent.
- **Challenger Loses:** The Challenger is exhausted and can’t declare another Gambit against the same Defender this round. The offered card is immediately arrested: turn it face-down and rotate it sideways. It can’t be fired this Autumn and resets to Active next round. The Defender’s Protective Ward Reagents are returned.

#### PROTECTIVE WARDS ON CRUCIBLE CARDS

During their turn in Phase 2: Summer, a player may place Reagents directly on any of their Active Crucible Cards as Protective Wards. These set the entry fee a Challenger must pay to declare a Gambit against that card. Protective Wards may not be placed reactively once a Gambit has been declared. There is no limit to the number of Reagents that may be placed as a Ward on a single card.
Ward Reagents are spent if the Challenger wins and returned if the Challenger loses. Ward Reagents are permanent once placed. They cannot be moved from the Crucible card until it is Fired. The Ward Reagents CAN be used to complete the card’s requirements. See Compendium: 1.11 Crucible & Philosopher’s Stone

### 1.11 CRUCIBLE & PHILOSOPHER’S STONE

Your Philosopher’s Stone begins at the Start position and must travel through the Crucible, advancing through four Stages of Transmutation before reaching the Altar of Kismeta at the center. The Stone may advance only one position per round.

#### THE CRUCIBLE: Forge, Mantle Ring, & Altar Of Kismeta

- **Forge:** The Forge (positions 1, 3, 5, 7) is where the four stages of the Transmutation process occur. The Stone must remain in the Forge for one full round per stage to complete the Transmutation process. While it is in the Forge, it is vulnerable to attack. Rival Alchemists may declare an Opposition to attempt to send it to a Stasis zone. See Compendium: 1.12 Opposition.
- **Mantle Ring:** The Mantle Ring (positions 0, 2, 4, 6) is where the Philosopher’s Stone safely rests, tempering between each stage of Transmutation. The Stone is safe in these locations.
- **Altar of Kismeta:** The Altar is the final resting place of the finalized Philosopher’s Stone. It sits at the very center of the Great Year board. The first player to reach this space wins the game and is deemed the “Magnus Alchemist.”

#### TRANSMUTATION: The Path Of Ascension

The board is numbered (0) to (8). The Stone must advance through all nine positions in order:

- (0) START: → Starting position.
- (1) LEAD: FORGE → Stage 1 in process. Stone is Forging; vulnerable to attack.
- (2) MANTLE RING → Stage 1 complete. Safe.
- (3) BRONZE: FORGE → Stage 2 in process. Stone is Forging; vulnerable to attack.
- (4) MANTLE RING → Stage 2 complete. Safe.
- (5) SILVER: FORGE → Stage 3 in process. Stone is Forging; vulnerable to attack.
- (6) MANTLE RING → Stage 3 complete. Safe.
- (7) GOLD: FORGE → Stage 4 in process. Stone is Forging; vulnerable to attack.
- (8) ALTAR OF KISMETA → Victory. The Great Work is complete.

#### PHILOSOPHER’S STONE STATES

As the Philosopher’s Stone journeys around the Great Year board, it passes through four states:

- **Resting:** The Stone is on the Mantle Ring (positions 0, 2, 4, or 6). It is safe from rival challenge and is between the stages of the Transmutation process.
- **Forging:** The Stone is in the Forge (positions 1, 3, 5, 7). The Transmutation is underway. Your Crucible Card is face-up and turned sideways (Fired). The Stone is vulnerable to Opposition.
- **In Stasis:** The Stone is seized by opponents, moved to a ❄️ Stasis zone on the Mantle Ring. The Crucible Card stays Fired. The Stone remains frozen until the next round.
- **Tempering:** The act of advancing the Stone from the Forge to the next Mantle Ring, completing the current Stage. The corresponding Crucible Card is discarded.

#### PROTECTIVE WARDS

When moving your Stone into the Forge, place Reagents on the 🛡️ symbol as Protective Wards. They set the Reagent cost that challengers must pay to oppose you. Protective Wards in the Forge follow these rules:

- Opposed and lose: Your Protective Wards are discarded to the supply.
- Opposed and win: Your Protective Wards remain in place for the next round.
- On successful Tempering: Any remaining Protective Wards are burned in the Forge and returned to the supply. They are not recovered.
- On a Crucible Card: Ward Reagents placed on a Crucible Card via Gambit defense can also be used to satisfy that card’s Alignment requirements while they remain on the card. See 1.10 Gambit.

#### RETURNING FROM STASIS

A Stone sent to Stasis is frozen until Phase 3: Autumn of the next round. You may then move it back to the Forge position it previously held, and:

- Re-arm your Protective Ward by placing Reagents beside your Stone.
- Wait a full round of Forging before you may Temper to the next Mantle Ring. The Crucible Card remains Fired.

If the position is occupied:  
If the Forge position your Stone previously held is now occupied by another player’s Stone, you have two options: remain in Stasis for another round, or declare a Stasis Opposition against the occupying Stone during Step 2 of the current Autumn phase.

#### STASIS OPPOSITION

A Stasis Opposition follows the same rules as a standard Opposition, but produces a different outcome for both players:

- **Challenger wins:** The two Stones swap positions. The Challenger’s Stone moves from Stasis into the contested Forge position. The Defender’s Stone is moved to the nearest Stasis zone; their Protective Wards are discarded and their Crucible Card remains Fired.
- **Challenger loses:** The Challenger’s Stone remains in Stasis for another round.

📌 **Note:** All other rules of a standard Opposition apply: the Challenger pays the Defender’s Protective Ward cost as the entry fee, both players use their full Spread and may contribute Hand cards, and card effects are resolved before scores are compared. See 1.12 Opposition.

### 1.12 OPPOSITION

When your Stone is in the Forge, it can be attacked. A rival may declare an Opposition, a contest that uses Aspect Alignments and card effects to determine the outcome. If the Challenger wins, the Defender’s Stone is sent to the ❄️ Stasis zone and must wait a round before retrying. A failed Opposition exhausts the Challenger, who cannot challenge the same Defender again that round.

#### WHEN AN OPPOSITION CAN BE DECLARED

An Opposition can be declared during Step 2: Opposition of Phase 3: Autumn, on each player’s own turn. A valid target is any Stone currently Forging in the Crucible (positions 1, 3, 5, or 7) that entered the Forge in a previous round. Oppositions cannot be declared against a Stone placed in the Forge during this round’s Autumn phase.
**Multiple Oppositions Against the Same Stone**

More than one player may declare an Opposition against the same Forging Stone in the same round. The following rules govern this sequence:

**Resolution Order:** Oppositions are resolved one at a time, in the order they are declared. Each Opposition is fully resolved — including all card effects and outcomes — before the next may be declared. If more than one player wishes to challenge the same Defender, the next Declaration follows clockwise turn order from the previous Challenger.

**The Besieged Bonus:** Each time the Defender wins an Opposition during the current round, they earn +1 cumulative Alignment Points for all subsequent Oppositions that same round. This bonus is automatic and requires no action from the Defender.

_A Defender who entered the Forge without Protective Wards accepts greater vulnerability to multiple challenges. Wards, the Besieged Bonus, Hand cards, and Arcanum effects are the Defender's tools — whether or not they choose to use them._

- Stasis Opposition: A player returning from Stasis whose previous Forge position is occupied may also declare an Opposition during Step 2. This is called a Stasis Opposition. It follows all the same rules as a standard Opposition, but its outcome is a position swap rather than a send-to-Stasis result: a Challenger win swaps the two Stones, placing the Challenger in the Forge and the Defender in Stasis; a Challenger loss leaves the Challenger in Stasis for another round. See 1.11 Returning from Stasis for full outcomes.

After the Defender completes a full round and moves their Stone to the next Mantle Ring, they complete the full stage of Transmutation.

- ⚠️ Important: You cannot target a Stone that entered the Forge this round.

#### THE OPPOSITION: Order Of Play

- **The Challenge:** The Challenger pays the bank a quantity of Reagents equal to the Defender’s Protective Ward. This is the entry fee for the Opposition. No Ward = no entry fee.
- **Cards in Play:** All cards currently in each player’s Spread participate in the Opposition automatically. Their Aspects are used to calculate Alignment Points, and any card effects relevant to Oppositions are active for the duration of the contest.
  - **Hand Cards:** You may also contribute cards from your Hand to the Opposition. Hand cards used this way are discarded after the Opposition is resolved.
  - **Arcanum:** Any Major Arcana card (Adept or Fate) currently in either player’s Arcanum zone may have active effects during the Opposition. Some Fate cards may affect the Opposition for both players. Check each card’s text.
  - **Opposition Effects:** Some card effects modify Opposition outcomes, adjusting Alignment Points, changing contest rules, or causing other results. These are resolved before final scores are compared. See individual card text.
- **The Roll:** The Opposition Age is the current round’s Cosmic Age, set at the start of Phase 1: Spring and known to all players throughout the round. Both players roll their Zodiac Die and place it on the corresponding Sign.
- **Calculating the Results:** All Alignment Points are totaled. The player with the higher score wins. **Tied Scores:** If both players have equal Alignment Points, each player rolls their Zodiac Die; the higher roll wins. Reroll if tied again.

#### CALCULATING THE ALIGNMENT POINTS

Each player’s Alignment Points are the sum of all Aspect matches across every source in play. Each source is scored independently, and all points are added together. See 1.1: Alignment Points for the full scoring table.
Sources for Alignments:

- Zodiac Dice
- Astral Houses
- Spread Cards (All cards in your Spread)
- Hand Cards (Played & discarded after use)
- Arcanum: Major Arcana (Adept & Fate)

Opposition Effects:  
Certain card effects specifically modify Opposition outcomes. These effects may adjust Alignment Points, alter the contest rules, or produce other results. All modifications are resolved before the outcome is decided.

⚙️ **Game Mode Modifier: (Magnus Alchemist)** If you are Misaligned with the Defender at the moment of declaration, add +1 to your final Alignment Points total before comparing results. The Defender receives no bonus. See GAME MODES: Magnus Alchemist.

#### OUTCOMES

- **Challenger Wins:** The Defender’s Stone is moved to the nearest ❄️ Stasis zone on the Mantle Ring. Their Protective Wards are discarded. Their Crucible Card remains Fired (face-up, turned sideways).
- **Defender Wins:** The Stone remains in the Forge. The Challenger is Exhausted and may not challenge the same Defender again for the remainder of the current round.

#### EXAMPLE: An Opposition

The current Cosmic Age is Leo (Sign: Leo, Planet: Sun, Element: Fire) and serves as the Opposition Age for this contest.
Opposition Age is Leo (Sign: Leo, Planet: Sun, Element: Fire).
You are the Challenger. Your rival’s Stone is currently forging at position 3 (Bronze).

Your Sources (Challenger):

- Zodiac Die result: Aries (Element: Fire): → Element match (Fire) = +1
- Astral House in Leo (Sign: Leo): → Sign match (Leo) = +3
- Activated card (Planet: Sun): → Planet match (Sun) = +2
- Your Alignment Points = 6

Defender’s Sources:

- Zodiac Die result: Cancer: → No Match = +0
- Astral House in Aries: (Element: Fire) → Element Match (Fire) = +1
- Activated Card: (Planet: Mars) → No Match = +0
- Active Adept Card: (+2 to Fire) → Element Match (Fire) = +2
- Defender’s Alignment Points = 3

Outcome:  
You (Challenger) win (6 > 3). The Defender’s Stone moves to the nearest ❄️ Stasis zone. Their Protective Wards are discarded, and their Crucible Card remains Fired.

### 1.13 OFFERING TO THE AGE

Each winter, as the Cosmic Age ends, the Agekeeper convenes all Alchemists to make an Offering to the Age. Players may choose to participate. If the Offering succeeds—by reaching a set Rank Point Threshold—everyone who contributes receives an Cauldron Catalyst from the Fates. This token grants a 2-for-1 refining boost for a specific Reagent type. When a player receives an Cauldron Catalyst, they place it in their chosen Cauldron to activate the bonus for that Reagent.

#### CALLING THE OFFERING

- The Agekeeper announces the Offering.
- Cauldron Catalysts return to the Altar of Kismeta at the center of the board.
- Contributions are declared openly around the table.
- Players may negotiate amongst themselves regarding how to complete the Offering.
- A player may offer any number of cards from their Spread or Hand.
- Major Arcana cards in the Arcanum zone are not eligible.
- Cards remain with each player until the Offering is settled.
- Only players who participate in the Offering receive the reward.

#### ELIGIBLE CARDS

A card is eligible for the Offering if it shares at least one Aspect with the current Cosmic Age: a Zodiac Sign, Planet, or Element/Suit match. Rank Points are determined by the card’s Rank.

#### ACCEPTING THE OFFERING: RP THRESHOLD

All contributed cards’ Rank Points are pooled. If the combined total meets or exceeds the Threshold for the current player count, the Offering succeeds.

- 2 Players: 25 RP combined | 3 Players: 35 RP combined | 4 Players: 45 RP combined

#### RESOLUTION

- **Threshold met:** Each contributing player immediately takes their Cauldron Catalyst from the Altar and places it in a Cauldron of their choice. Contributed cards are discarded.
- **Threshold not met:** All Cauldron Catalysts remain on the Altar. Cards remain with players.
- ⚠️ Important: There is no partial success. If the threshold is not reached, no player earns the boon.

#### THE REWARD: Cauldron Catalyst

A Cauldron Catalyst, grants 2-for-1 crafting for that Cauldron’s Reagent for the full following round. The Cauldron’s RP Threshold applies as normal.

- ⚙️ Game Mode Modifier: (Magnus Alchemist) The Cauldron Catalyst must be placed in the Cauldron that matches the Element of the concluded Cosmic Age.

### 1.14 FATEFUL WAGER

At each round's end, Alchemists can wager on a Zodiac Sign to predict the next Cosmic Age. Correct guesses yield rewards; incorrect ones forfeit the bet to the Fates.

#### PLACING A WAGER

- Wager as many cards as you like on a single Zodiac Sign.
- Place your Meeple on top of the cards to track each player’s bets.
- Cannot wager Major Arcana cards from the Arcanum zone.
- Wagers are resolved next round, when the Agekeeper rolls the Cosmic Age Die.
- **Bet Correctly:** Your wagered cards are doubled and returned to you.
- **Bet Incorrectly:** Your wagered cards are lost.
